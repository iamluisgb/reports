COBRA-Skills: Contextual Bandit-Guided Evolution for Agent Skill Optimization
Submissions del 10 Sep — ArXiv no publica en fin de semana. Casts agent skill optimization as budgeted sequential search: a contextual bandit decides which candidate skills are worth evaluating, cutting optimization cost 55–58% versus SkillOpt with only 50 examples per benchmark while topping six agent benchmarks. [agents, skills, bandits]
arXiv
02
X-AuT: Progressive Audio-Encoder Compression for Speech LLMs with Cross-Scale Distillation
Prunes speech-LLM audio encoders layer by layer, picking layer combinations with short behavioral probes and restoring quality via cross-scale distillation: Qwen3-ASR-0.6B sheds 20.7% of its audio-tower parameters at 14 layers for just 0.14 points of extra error. [speech, compression, efficiency]
arXiv
03
Beyond Confidence: Stability-Aware Test-Time Adaptation for LLM Reasoning
Observes that high-confidence reasoning is only trustworthy when confidence stays stable under local perturbations, and builds TASCO on that: test-time adaptation that optimizes a lightweight task prefix with the LLM frozen, improving reasoning accuracy and token efficiency. [reasoning, test-time, inference]
arXiv
04
Biology-in-the-loop: Amortized Adaptive Hit Discovery in CRISPR Screens
AssayBench-Loop, a benchmark of 1,389 CRISPR screens for adaptive hit discovery, plus AssayLoop: a transformer amortized over historical screens decides what to assay next, reaching 5.67× enrichment over random selection and recovering 27.7% of hits after testing ~5% of the library. [AI-for-science, benchmark, experimental-design]

Explainability Assistant: A Conversational XAI Interface for Interpreting Energy Consumption Models
A conversational XAI system that uses LLM function calling to let building operators interrogate energy-forecasting models in natural language — 94% intent-parsing accuracy versus 76.8% for the previous custom-grammar approach, and every expert in the evaluation preferred it to a dashboard.
