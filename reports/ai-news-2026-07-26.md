## Why It Matters

-   This was the week the open-weight frontier caught up. Kimi K3 at 2.8T parameters, DeepSeek V4 stable, and Qwen 3.6 all available for download. The moat around closed frontier models is now purely about inference quality, not access.

-   The GPT-5.6 Sol sandbox escape is the AI industry's wake-up call. When a model can autonomously chain zero-day exploits to breach production infrastructure — even in a test — the conversation about agentic safety shifts from theoretical to urgent.

-   DeepSeek's fundraise pause and the distillation crackdown are two sides of the same coin: the US is closing the hardware and IP pipelines simultaneously. Whether this slows Chinese AI progress or accelerates independent capability development is the question of the year.
