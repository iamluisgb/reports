MindTopo: Can Foundation Models Reason in Topological Space?
Submissions from 10 Sep — ArXiv does not publish on weekends. MindTopo benchmarks topological intuition across continuity, separation, order, enclosure and knots at reasoning and closed-loop planning levels, with 11,030 procedurally generated instances; across 14 MLLMs, reasoning beats planning and the best model stays far below human performance. [benchmark, spatial-reasoning, eval]
arXiv
02
Domain-Specific Hallucination Detection in Large Language Models
A response-level detection pipeline combining a fine-tuned DeBERTa-v3 classifier, MC Dropout uncertainty and temperature-scaled calibration reaches F1=0.915 / AUROC=0.977 on HaluEval, while DPO on a Qwen2.5-0.5B generator cuts its hallucination rate from 85.5% to 37.7%; transfer to the biomedical SciFact benchmark is weak (F1=0.52). [hallucination, calibration, eval]
arXiv
03
RetroThinker: Enabling Retrospective Thinking in Speech LLMs
RetroThinker post-trains the streaming Moshi model to self-verify and forward-correct its chain-of-thought while the user is still speaking, pairing SFT on retrospective data with length-based DPO; on GSM8K it reports an 11% absolute accuracy gain at comparable latency. [speech, reasoning, dpo]
arXiv
04
RAG-Safety-Bench: Reliable Evaluation of Retrieval-Augmented LLM Safety
A benchmark that isolates retrieval's effect on safety across four conditions — no RAG, an oracle document, related-but-answerless documents, and random safe documents — and across five open-source LLMs finds an inverse relationship between benign and unsafe capability, with baseline guardrails giving no downstream safety guarantee. [rag, safety, benchmark]
arXiv
05
Nuha-Speech: Building General-Purpose Arabic Speech-LLMs
Nuha-Speech builds a 1.5M-sample Arabic speech question-answering corpus, instruction-tunes Qwen-Omni variants at several scales on it, and adds a task-and-metric evaluation framework for Arabic speech LLMs. [speech, multilingual, fine-tuning]
arXiv
06
Negative Self-Distillation: Learning to Reason by Avoiding Flaws
NSD replaces on-policy self-distillation's imitation of privileged traces with divergence from a self-generated flawed "careless reasoner", gating updates so they hit reasoning-critical tokens only, and outperforms OPSD and label-free RL baselines. [self-distillation, reasoning, training]
