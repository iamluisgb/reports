01
COBRA-Skills: Contextual Bandit-Guided Evolution for Agent Skill Optimization
COBRA-Skills treats agent skill optimization as budgeted sequential search: a contextual bandit decides which candidate skills are worth an execution run, and evidence-grounded evolution keeps refining the skill pool from the feedback. Across six agent benchmarks and three models it tops the comparison while cutting optimization cost 55–58% versus SkillOpt, using only 50 examples per benchmark. [agents, skills, contextual-bandit]
arXiv
02
Ecdysis: Efficient and Effective Training of Runtime Harnesses for LLM Agents
Ecdysis trains runtime harnesses for LLM agents instead of evolving them by brute-force search: it aggregates failures across task instances to separate model-specific misses from systematic harness defects, then repairs only the harness. It reports up to 1.84x faster harness training and +18.56% reasoning accuracy over prior harness-evolution methods. [agents, harness, failure-analysis]
arXiv
03
Beyond Confidence: Stability-Aware Test-Time Adaptation for LLM Reasoning
TASCO adds stability to confidence-guided test-time adaptation: high confidence predicts correctness only when it survives local perturbations, so a lightweight task-level prefix is optimized under random or sharpness-aware perturbations while the LLM stays frozen, improving reasoning accuracy and token efficiency. [reasoning, test-time-adaptation, efficiency]
arXiv
04
X-AuT: Progressive Audio-Encoder Compression for Speech LLMs with Cross-Scale Distillation
X-AuT compresses speech-LLM audio encoders progressively: short behavioral probes pick which layer combinations survive, and cross-scale distillation plus LoRA finetuning restore quality with the backbone frozen. Pruning Qwen3-ASR-0.6B from 18 to 14 audio-encoder layers keeps error near baseline while dropping 20.7% of audio-tower parameters. [speech, compression, distillation]
arXiv
05
Biology-in-the-loop: Amortized Adaptive Hit Discovery in CRISPR Screens
AssayBench-Loop packages 1,389 CRISPR screens as a benchmark for adaptive hit discovery, and AssayLoop learns an amortized acquisition policy across historical experiments, handing off to LLM-derived biological priors. On held-out screens it recovers 27.7% of hits after assaying ~5% of the library — a 5.67x enrichment over random selection. [benchmark, bio-ml, experimental-design]
arXiv
06
Negative Self-Distillation: Learning to Reason by Avoiding Flaws
NSD replaces on-policy self-distillation's imitation of privileged traces with divergence from a self-generated flawed "careless reasoner", gating updates so they hit reasoning-critical tokens only, and outperforms OPSD and label-free RL baselines. [self-distillation, reasoning, training]
