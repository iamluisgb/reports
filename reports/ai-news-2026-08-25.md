## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### PhysElite: How Far Are LLMs from Solving Olympiad-Level Physics Problems?

A bilingual multimodal benchmark of 11,586 Olympiad-tier physics problems with diagrams and step-by-step solutions; of 18 MLLMs tested, the strongest answers only 33.7% correctly. **[benchmark, multimodal, reasoning]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25097v1)

### The Von-Neumann State-Space Transformer for neural decoding

Replaces the shared feed-forward operator with a low-rank instruction bank selected per token from a low-dimensional state-space memory, beating a modern Transformer on three motor-cortex decoding benchmarks. **[architecture, efficiency, neuroscience]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25088v1)

### Tunable Tool-Call Rates in LLM Agents via Representation Steering

A single linear direction in the residual stream, extracted without training, drives an agent's tool-call rate from near 0% to over 90% and nearly doubles open-domain QA accuracy under live tool execution. **[agents, tool-use, steering]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25198v1)

### FLARE: Verifying MILP Reformulations with LLM-Based Theorem Proving

An LLM agent plus the Lean proof assistant checks that MILP reformulations preserve the original problem, emitting a machine-checkable certificate for every accepted case and hitting 100% on the NP-hard subset of FormulationBench. **[verification, optimization, theorem-proving]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25220v1)

### MTDiag: A Multi-Turn Diagnostic Dataset Towards Clinically Meaningful LLM Evaluation

Builds multi-turn diagnostic dialogues from DDXPlus, MIMIC-IV and published case reports, with physician-validated utterances and knowledge-grounded metrics that go beyond diagnostic accuracy. **[eval, medical, multi-turn]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25085v1)
