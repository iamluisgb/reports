## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### MERIT: Mitigating Exposure Bias in Generative XMC for User-Interest Propensity Modeling

Aims at generative extreme multi-label matching in e-commerce, where an autoregressive model conditions on its own early mistakes and over-produces correlated labels. MERIT adds a permutation-invariant multi-target loss over shuffled gold and mined hard negatives, reporting at least 11.9% higher global recall on a 250k+ category dataset and +0.26% conversion in production A/B tests. **[retrieval, recsys, training]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28931v1)

### Hybrid Offline-Online Multi-Agent Decision Transformers for Wireless Resource Management

Pretrains a multi-agent decision transformer offline by supervised sequence modeling, then fine-tunes online with critic-guided gradients, return-weighted sampling and critics conditioned on neighbors' actions. Both training and execution stay distributed on local observations, reaching QoS comparable to centralized methods on joint scheduling plus power allocation and coordinated beamforming. **[agents, reinforcement-learning, wireless]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28878v1)

### A rigor-matched audit of periodic-step layer skipping for efficient LLM inference

Three-seed comparison of ConfLayers, SWIFT and vanilla autoregressive decoding on Qwen2.5-0.5B and 1.5B across GSM8K and CNN/DailyMail. SWIFT wins accuracy in three of four cells and is 5-21% faster once online-search overhead is separated from inference cost, reversing the naive wall-clock ranking; ConfLayers is dominated everywhere. Trained routers tested as a supplement give 1.08-1.33x speedups at much lower accuracy. **[inference, efficiency, evaluation]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28846v1)

### Capability-Stratified Degradation in Ternary Language Models

Converts Qwen3.5-0.8B to ternary weights with 72.4M tokens of quantization-aware training and evaluates 29 benchmarks plus representation probes. A linear probe recovers 43.76% of MMLU answers from the teacher's representations but only 26.19% from the ternary model, while ten tasks average 77.1% of teacher performance and fine-tuning lifts SST-2 to 89.8% of the matched teacher. **[quantization, inference, evaluation]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28809v1)

### ERR+: Sequential Entropy Resolution for Efficient and Decisive LLM Reasoning

Notes that correct reasoning traces show more frequent and larger token-level entropy drops inside the thinking phase than incorrect ones, then rewards that resolution directly instead of suppressing entropy. A second phase scores response length against co-generated peers, and the two rewards are applied sequentially because joint optimization conflicts early in training. **[reasoning, RLVR, inference]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28771v1)

## Why It Matters

-   The day's AI news is mostly institutional, not technical: sanctions against a collective, a court striking down the blacklisting of a frontier lab, and a Chinese lab putting flagship-class weights out for free. Each one changes who can be cut off from a model, and the open-weight release is the cheapest hedge available against the other two.

-   Three of today's papers measure where efficiency tricks break: a rigor-matched layer-skipping audit, a ternary quantization post-mortem, and an entropy-based reasoning reward. The shared finding is that headline speedups usually come with capability that is unevenly, not uniformly, degraded — which is exactly what a benchmark average hides.
