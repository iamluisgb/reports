Why It Matters
▸ Agent security is maturing from research to production. Three papers today (MCP Poisoning, LivePI, MemFail) converge on the same insight: as LLM agents gain tool access and persistent memory, the attack surface explodes beyond traditional API boundaries. The Agent Trace RFC is the industry's first attempt at standardization — auditability before regulation.
▸ Infrastructure for LLMs is becoming its own discipline. MinT (LoRA serving at scale) and the 504-GPU operational analysis paper show that the bottleneck has shifted from model architecture to systems engineering — reliability, adapter management, and hardware failure recovery are the new SRE frontier.
▸ Anthropic's enterprise expansion (Fujitsu + legal plugins) signals the shift from "AI as a tool" to "AI as infrastructure." Claude Cowork is becoming the middleware layer between enterprise systems and LLM reasoning — similar to how Docker became the container layer.
