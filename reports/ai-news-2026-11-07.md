## Papers — ArXiv

### FlashOffloading: Low-Latency KV Cache Offloading for Long-Context LLM Serving

Long-context serving hits the HBM capacity wall as KV cache grows with sequence length and concurrency; naive offloading to host DRAM adds management and H2D load overhead that eats the throughput win. FlashOffloading makes host DRAM the centre of the architecture — rolling HBM cache for prefill, hot cache for decode, a reverse sparse index with zero extra miss-lookup overhead, and a compute–load overlapped kernel across the whole pipeline. The result is offloading that raises concurrency without inflating latency. **[kv-cache, inference-serving, memory-hierarchy]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07542v1)

### Beyond the Agent Harness: Boundary Criteria for Software-Factory Orchestration

A layer above the coding-agent harness that orchestrates many runs into continuous delivery now exists in several systems, but nobody agrees on which mechanism belongs where. The paper proposes boundary criteria anchored on an operational definition of a run: single-run mechanisms are harness, cross-run coordination and state handoff are factory harness, domain-coupled logic is the domain layer. Failures get coded across three fields — where the defect originated, which control missed it, where it was fixed — with positive evidence required before blaming the environment. A single-operator case study grounds the taxonomy. **[agent-orchestration, harnesses, failure-analysis]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06946v1)

### Benchmarking Large Language Models on Heterogeneous Cognitive and Biomedical Knowledge Graphs

Whether LLMs reason reliably over heterogeneous knowledge graphs is unclear, so the authors benchmark five models — GPT-5.1, Gemini 2.0 Pro, Llama 3, Qwen, BioGPT — across five KGs under graph-grounded and KG-agnostic conditions. General-purpose models beat the domain-specific ones on multi-hop reasoning, with performance degrading as query complexity rises. KG grounding cuts hallucination rates across every model tested; inter-annotator agreement was substantial at Cohen's kappa 0.781. **[knowledge-graphs, hallucination, benchmarking]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08315v1)

### From Single-Agent Self-Discovery to Multi-Agent Adaptation: Scaling Laws of Partner Feedback and Convention Rigidity in Meta RL

Meta-learned update rules like Disco103 and OPEN replace hand-crafted optimisers, but nobody knew whether co-evolution with adapting teammates sustains gains or traps agents in brittle conventions. Across 1,800+ runs in VMAS and Overcooked, the paper establishes five laws of multi-agent adaptation. The headline finding is an evaluation illusion: counterfactual partner swaps show apparent team progress masking focal regression (+1.066 team gain concealing a −0.119 focal loss) and inflating transfer by up to 1.210. A 288-run sweep yields a linear scaling law for live reciprocal feedback keeping self-discovered learners adaptive. **[multi-agent, meta-learning, evaluation]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01742v1)

### CST-Bench: Benchmarking Counterfactual Spatial Transition Reasoning

Multimodal LLM benchmarks mostly test static scenes; CST-Bench tests what happens when objects are moved or swapped and the model must infer how the intervention changes scene state. It ships 7,082 examples across 6 intervention types and 12 transformation operators, with dense causal annotations — intervention sources, affected relations, expected effects, invariants. Post-training methods that work on ordinary spatial reasoning still fail under counterfactual interventions. **[spatial-reasoning, counterfactuals, multimodal]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07541v1)

### NPCBench: A Clinical Apprenticeship Benchmark for Multimodal Care-Pathway Reasoning

Medical benchmarks score isolated answers, which reveals little about whether a model can track a patient across a course of care. NPCBench mirrors specialist training for nasopharyngeal carcinoma: 4,777 items from guideline knowledge through case planning to longitudinal review, plus a 100-patient CarePath cohort with 417 scored tasks. Episode-level evaluation requires diagnostic and care answers to be jointly correct within the pathway, not item by item. **[medical-ai, benchmarks, long-horizon]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08369v1)

### Eigenius: typed knowledge that remembers how a claim was established

A theory for persistent typed-knowledge stores where proof irrelevance is the enemy: the store must retain who established a claim, by which derivation or tool, and under which assumptions. Directly relevant to provenance in LLM-built knowledge bases.

### When better confidence estimates fail to improve a knowledge-graph audit

Better error prediction in LLM-built KGs can buy almost nothing in a human audit. The paper models auditing as budgeted covering and characterises the decision-relevant error statistics — sharper confidence is not the bottleneck.

## Why It Matters

-   The strongest signal today cuts across the Hightouch judge write-up and the harness-boundary paper: the money and the defects both live between layers, not inside them. Hightouch gets Opus-level accuracy at a quarter of the cost by deciding which cases deserve escalation; the cs.SE paper formalises the same discipline as taxonomy — which run a mechanism belongs to, which control failed to stop a defect, with positive evidence required before blaming the environment. Teams shipping agents without this vocabulary are debugging by vibes.

-   The serving story is diverging from the model story. FlashOffloading argues the binding constraint on long-context inference is the memory hierarchy, not the model — which is why a Flash-tier release like Gemini Omni 1.2 ships with no benchmarks and gets evaluated in a 225-comment thread instead. Meanwhile Anthropic's IPO buyers floating $1.1tn after this week's rout is the market pricing the gap between model capability claims and the unglamorous infrastructure that actually delivers them. Read the Firecracker escape the same way: the substrate under your multi-tenant inference is attack surface regardless of which model sits on top.

-   The knowledge-graph cluster is quietly converging on an uncomfortable conclusion. Graph grounding measurably cuts hallucination rates, and Eigenius argues provenance — who established a claim, under which assumptions — must be retained. But the audit paper shows sharper confidence estimates buy almost nothing in a human audit, because the bottleneck is decision-relevant error statistics, not score quality. Better predictions are not the same as better decisions; anyone building LLM-curated knowledge bases should spend the engineering budget on what auditors actually need to act on.
