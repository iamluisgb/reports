## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### HBQ: Hierarchical Scaling Block Quantization with Hardware-Efficiency-Aware Design for Accurate LLM Inference

Large-block quantization with low-overhead significand scaling reaches W4A16-level accuracy at W4A5 on a 28nm ASIC, with up to 4.6x higher area/energy efficiency than state-of-the-art weight-only quantization. **[inference, quantization, hardware]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00450v1)

### Towards a Belief-Based World Model for LLM Agents

Instead of only simulating candidate actions, agents query a maintained belief about the current state; exposing those beliefs improves decision-making under partial observability and complements simulation-based world models. **[agents, world-models, planning]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00455v1)

### RestoreBench: Can AI Agents Restore Power Flow Convergence?

A benchmark that pits chatbot, single-agent and multi-agent LLM systems against 92 non-convergent power-flow cases across two grids, with defined observation, action and scoring schemes. **[agents, benchmark, tool-use]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00384v1)

### EGT-KG: Evidence-Grounded Typed KG Retrieval for Practical Scientific QA with Small Language Models

Typed knowledge-graph retrieval beats vanilla RAG for local small models on a scientific QA benchmark, with the best gain from llama3:8b at +14.67% final score. **[RAG, knowledge-graph, small-models]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00479v1)

### Validity-Aware Jailbreak Evaluation for Large Language Models

SEAV decomposes responses into ordered steps and checks validity and correctness, cutting false positives on a strategic-dishonesty diagnostic by 14.9pp and reclassifying 22–51% of previously labeled jailbreak successes as invalid. **[safety, eval, jailbreak]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00498v1)

## Why It Matters

-   With Manifest V2 finally gone from the Chrome Web Store, uBlock Origin's removal turns a years-long policy fight into a migration decision: ad-blocking capability in Chrome now depends on platform approval, and the practical answer for many users is a different browser.

-   Apple being caught off guard by AI-driven Mac Mini and Mac Studio demand is a hardware demand signal for local inference — the same machines developers buy to run models locally — and it arrives without a corresponding supply plan.
