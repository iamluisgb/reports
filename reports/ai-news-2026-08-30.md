## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### Budget-Aware Compression Pipeline for Single-GPU LLM Inference

Treats single-GPU 70B serving as a budget split across pruning, quantization and KV-cache compression; the pipeline fits a 70B model in ~33 GB and holds ~57 tok/s on 10k-token prompts on one A40. **[inference, quantization, serving]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30076v2)

### Reachability-Based Capability Confinement for LLM Agents under Indirect Prompt Injection

SkillGuard treats untrusted skill output as state contamination and clamps an agent's future authority via a Skill Impact Graph and an inline reference monitor, eliminating tool-knowledge attacks on three of four AgentDojo suites. **[agents, prompt-injection, security]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30041v1)

### AutoCRAT: Within-trajectory Joint Control of Stochasticity and Compute for LLM Reasoning

A decoder-side controller that adjusts sampling stochasticity and reasoning budget inside a single trajectory, cutting inference tokens 13.8-52.7% over static configs while gaining 1.5-4.5% relative accuracy across 6 benchmarks. **[reasoning, inference, decoding]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29988v1)

### Generative vs. Encoder Models for Multilingual NER: A Comprehensive Empirical Study on Naamapadam

Across 11 Indic languages, fine-tuned encoders (mBERT, XLM-R) beat every generative architecture in 10 of 11 languages by 7.5-40 points, and the best few-shot result reaches only 28% of the encoder baseline. **[NER, multilingual, eval]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29959v1)

### SearchWiki: Learning to Build and Navigate Knowledge Wikis for Active Information Seeking

Synthesizes a corpus into a hierarchical, typed wiki and RL-trains a 9B agent to navigate it over multiple turns, beating same-size baselines and matching larger models on ViDoRe-V3, FinanceBench and memory benchmarks. **[RAG, agents, retrieval]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29953v1)

## Why It Matters

-   Two of the day's biggest threads are the same argument at different altitudes: California writes an exemption keyed to how code is licensed and distributed, while ProtectEU pursues obligations that ignore distribution entirely. Whoever owns the build pipeline inherits the compliance question, which is why both land on engineering desks rather than legal ones.

-   The arXiv batch is a reminder that most deployable gains are still budget allocation problems — pruning against quantization, KV-cache sparsification against INT8, capability clamps against agent utility — not new architectures. AutoCRAT and SkillGuard both show the pattern of spending less of something (tokens, authority) rather than adding capability.
