## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### Eliciting Self-Verification in Multimodal Reasoning Agents with Reinforcement Learning

An RL-only framework trains multimodal agents to verify and filter retrieved evidence inside their own traces, gaining multi-hop VQA accuracy and tool efficiency from 5K examples. **[agents, RL, multimodal]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08025v1)

### SAFIRE: Safety-Critical Benchmark for Fine-grained Fire and Smoke Understanding in Multimodal LLMs

An 83K-image, 193K-question benchmark for fire and smoke understanding finds ten open MLLMs averaging 61.9% accuracy on safety-critical reasoning. **[benchmark, safety, multimodal]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07823v1)

### TDDN: Text-aligned Diffused DINO Network for Puzzle Understanding

Fuses DINOv3 and CleanDIFT into a text-aligned encoder that more than triples CLIP's dense-prediction accuracy using far less training data. **[vision, representation, eval]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07937v1)

### A*-Thought-V2: Efficient Latent Reasoning via Geometric Dynamics of LLM

Compresses chain-of-thought steps that deviate from the question-to-solution direction into latent tokens, cutting response length by half while gaining up to 2.6% accuracy. **[reasoning, inference, efficiency]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07821v1)

### VEX-Bench: Benchmarking LLM Agents for Assessing Exploitability of Software Supply Chain Vulnerabilities

The first benchmark for judging whether known dependency vulnerabilities are actually exploitable: only GPT-5.5 clears 70% macro-F1 on fine-grained justifications. **[agents, security, eval]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08040v1)

## Why It Matters

-   The two most-upvoted stories of the day were both about control: a TV that keeps listening with the screen off, and an archive asking users to fund its own survival. Consumer-hardware trust and independent infrastructure are the same fight from opposite ends.

-   The agent benchmarks and the business simulation converge on a sober conclusion: autonomy fails at accounting, verification and exploitability assessment, not at language. That gap between fluent output and checked output is where the next round of tooling has to land.
