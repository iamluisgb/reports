## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### SEED: Self-Evolving On-Policy Distillation for Agentic Reinforcement Learning

A self-evolving distillation method where an agent model generates its own training data through on-policy interaction, then distills knowledge back into itself — continuous improvement without external supervision. **[agent, rl]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14777)

### SearchOS-V1: Towards Robust Open-Domain Information-Seeking Agent Collaboration

Multi-agent collaboration framework for open-domain information seeking, addressing robustness in agent-to-agent coordination when searching across diverse knowledge bases. **[agent, ai]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15257)

### LongStraw: Long-Context RL Beyond 2M Tokens under a Fixed GPU Budget

Extends reinforcement learning to handle contexts exceeding 2M tokens without requiring additional GPU resources — critical for long-document reasoning and agent memory. **[rl, llm]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14952)

### VideoChat3: Fully Open Video MLLM for Efficient and Generalist Video Understanding

Fully open-source video multimodal LLM achieving generalist video understanding with efficient architecture — contributes to the growing open model ecosystem. **[llm, multimodal]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14935)

### RxBrain: Embodied Cognition Foundation Model with Joint Language-Visual Reasoning

A foundation model for embodied cognition integrating language and visual reasoning with imagination capabilities — towards agents that can reason about physical reality. **[reasoning, ai]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14187)

### Smarter and Cheaper at Once: Byte-Exact KV-Cache Grafting

A frozen small model augmented with byte-exact KV cache grafting from a larger model at inference time — achieving larger-model quality at small-model cost. Practical inference optimization. **[model, inference]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14431)

### BadWAM: When World-Action Models Dream Right but Act Wrong

Systematic analysis of failure modes in world-action models that can predict consequences accurately (dream right) but still produce incorrect actions — crucial for reliable agent deployment. **[model, rl]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15207)

### Partition, Prompt, Aggregate: Statistical Self-Consistency in Language Models

A statistically grounded approach to self-consistency that partitions reasoning paths, prompts for diversity, and aggregates across partitions — improving LLM answer reliability. **[model]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15277)

## Why It Matters

-   The Anthropic·Blackstone Ode venture signals that the AI industry's value capture is shifting from frontier model capabilities to *implementation infrastructure* — deployment, integration, and operational excellence become the moat. For an SRE, this means agent orchestration, cost governance, and reliability at the integration layer are where the real engineering leverage lives.

-   Agent Zero v1's plugin-first, Git-backed model and the SEED self-evolving distillation paper both point to the same trend: *self-improving agents with inspectable internals*. The tension between autonomy and observability is the defining engineering challenge of the agent era.
