## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### A Red-Team Study of Anthropic Fable 5 & Opus 4.8 Models

Evaluates adversarial robustness of two frontier Anthropic models against four families of automated jailbreak attacks across 7,826 harmful intents. Using the HackAgent red-teaming framework, the study provides a systematic benchmark of safety mechanisms in Anthropic's latest models. **[arXiv 16 Jun 2026]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18193v1)

### Variable-Width Transformers

Challenges the constant-width assumption in transformer architectures. Proposes allocating different widths across layers, showing that variable-width designs can match or exceed fixed-width performance at lower computational cost. **[arXiv 16 Jun 2026]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18246v1)

### Multi-Source Cybersecurity Logs: ATT&CK-Labeled Dataset and SLM Evaluation

Introduces a labeled multi-source dataset spanning system, network, and browser logs for detecting multi-stage cyberattacks. Evaluates small language models for cross-source event correlation — directly relevant for SIEM/Splunk use cases. **[arXiv 16 Jun 2026]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18190v1)

### Learning from the Self-future: On-policy Self-distillation for dLLMs

Applies on-policy self-distillation to diffusion LLMs, showing that privileged information can be injected through future-token conditioning rather than traditional left-to-right prefix masking. Extends OPSD beyond autoregressive architectures. **[arXiv 16 Jun 2026]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18195v1)

### Zone of Proximal Policy Optimization: Teacher in Prompts, Not Gradients

Proposes ZPPO, a knowledge distillation method that uses prompts (not logit-matching) to transfer teacher competence to small student models. Addresses the brittleness of standard distillation in the small-student regime. **[arXiv 16 Jun 2026]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18216v1)

### Evaluating Open-Source LLMs for Multi-Label ATT&CK Technique Classification

Benchmarks open-source LLMs for classifying Cyber Threat Intelligence reports into MITRE ATT&CK techniques. Compares zero-shot, few-shot, and fine-tuned approaches for automating threat intelligence pipelines. **[arXiv 16 Jun 2026]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18166v1)
