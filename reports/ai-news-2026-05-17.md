# The *AI* Briefing

17 May 2026 · Sunday

Anthropic nears $900B · OpenAI's $4B consulting play · The Apple rift deepens

## Headlines

### Anthropic agrees $30B funding at $900B valuation

Anthropic has agreed terms for a $30 billion funding round that would value the company at approximately $900 billion — roughly 2.5× its $380B valuation just three months ago. Sources say the deal could close as soon as this month. Initial reports suggested a target as high as $950B.

Sources: [FT](https://www.ft.com/content/9deae3c6-716d-4f4d-8b09-434d8519f847) [NYT](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/05/12/technology/anthropic-funding-950-billion-valuation.html) [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/anthropic-targets-900-billion-valuation-220005674.html)

### OpenAI creates $4B enterprise deployment company

OpenAI launched the OpenAI Deployment Company — a standalone enterprise consulting and integration business backed by $4 billion in initial investment with TPG and other private equity firms. It acquired applied-AI engineering firm Tomoro to staff the venture from day one, targeting HR and workflow transformation.

Sources: [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-creates-new-unit-with-4-billion-investment-aid-corporate-ai-push-2026-05-11/) [CIO Dive](https://www.ciodive.com/news/openai-deployment-company-4-billion-ai-consulting-integration/819942/) [HR Executive](https://hrexecutive.com/openais-new-4b-consulting-venture-lands-squarely-in-hr-territory/)

### Apple-OpenAI relationship frays — possible legal action

OpenAI is reportedly considering legal action against Apple over their ChatGPT integration, which failed to deliver the subscriber growth and visibility OpenAI expected. The two-year-old partnership is now deteriorating, with Bloomberg reporting the AI startup is preparing for a potential legal fight.

Sources: [NYT](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/05/14/technology/openai-apple-legal-action.html) [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-14/openai-apple-partnership-frays-setting-up-possible-legal-fight) [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/14/openai-is-reportedly-preparing-legal-action-against-apple-it-wouldnt-be-the-first-partner-to-feel-burned/)

### Anthropic secures massive compute deals: AWS + Google + SpaceX

Anthropic announced compute capacity agreements with three hyperscalers: up to 5 GW with AWS (including ~1 GW by end of 2026), a 5 GW deal with Google, and an additional agreement with SpaceX — signaling an unprecedented scaling race for frontier AI infrastructure.

Sources: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/higher-limits-spacex)

### OpenAI to spend $50B on compute in 2026 — may raise more capital

CTO Mira Murati (via CFO statements) confirmed OpenAI plans to spend $50 billion on computing power this year. The CFO also indicated the company may need to raise additional capital as the compute crunch deepens, building on the $122B round closed in March at $852B post-money valuation.

Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-05/openai-to-spend-50-billion-on-computing-in-2026-brockman-says) [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/accelerating-the-next-phase-ai/)

### PwC deploys Claude across enterprise functions

PwC announced it is deploying Claude across its services to build technology, execute deals, and reinvent enterprise functions for clients — signaling growing professional-services adoption of frontier AI models.

Sources: [Anthropic Newsroom](https://www.anthropic.com/news)
