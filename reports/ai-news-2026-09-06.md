## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### PARSER: Read in Parallel, Reason in Depth for Long-Context LLM Agents

Decouples reading from reasoning: frozen subagents own one chunk each while a lead agent runs scatter–gather rounds, beating the strongest sequential memory baseline by 5.7 points and cutting latency up to 11x. **[long-context, agents, RAG]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06702v1)

### NormViz: A Benchmark and Framework for Grounding Multimodal Reasoning in Global Cultures

Introduces NormViz-Bench, 3,268 contrastive image pairs across 16 countries for judging local social norms, where the strongest VLMs score below 27%, plus a 64K-image training set that lifts pair accuracy by up to 125%. **[multimodal, eval, culture]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06831v1)

### Typed Federated Artifacts for the Agentic Web: Sharing Tool-Routing Knowledge Across Frozen, Heterogeneous LLM Agents

Proposes schema-validated artifacts that let frozen models from different vendors exchange tool-routing knowledge, routing within 1.1 points of a centralized compendium on StableToolBench. **[agents, tool-use, federated]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06815v1)

### A Novel Semantic Manifold Alignment Attack against Embedding-to-Embedding Obfuscation in Privacy-Preserving LLMs

Proxy Manifold Alignment treats the obfuscated vector stream as an unknown language and aligns its manifold to a public corpus, recovering more plaintext than prior attacks on embedding-level privacy schemes. **[privacy, security, embeddings]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06749v1)

### Data Efficient Sample Selection for In-Context Learning

DearICL frames demonstration selection as a subset-ranking problem solved with a gap-index bandit, gaining 8.08–15.9% accuracy over linear bandit baselines at low sample complexity. **[in-context, data-selection, bandits]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06670v1)
