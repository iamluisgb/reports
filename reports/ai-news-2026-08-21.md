## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### Forgotten in Weights, Recovered by Tools: Agentic Tool Unlearning for LLM Agents

Unlearning in tool-augmented agents can fail quietly: suppressed parametric recall may be recovered via web search or database lookup. ATU pairs parametric unlearning with trajectory-level RL that penalizes target-seeking tool behavior, evaluated on RWKU and MUSE. **[agents, unlearning]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21544v1)

### presto: Efficient, Training-free, and Open-world Object Placement via Imaginary Search

Reformulates open-world object placement as training-free search inside a multimodal LLM's imaginary action space, refining position and scale coarse-to-fine; human raters preferred the MLLM-as-a-judge variant over metric-guided selection. **[multimodal, zero-shot]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21543v1)

### Measuring Activation Control in Large Language Models

A benchmark quantifying how well LLMs can steer their own residual-stream activations via natural-language instruction; in simple tasks that control can evade activation-based monitors such as linear probes, a confound for latent-space safety monitoring. **[safety, interpretability]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21664v1)

### Mitigating Database Leakage in RAG Systems with Keyword-Grounded Fact Substitution

KFS-RAG defends against prompt-injection-driven database leakage by identifying influential keywords in retrieved context and substituting raw passages with keyword-grounded facts, cutting leakage while preserving answer accuracy. **[RAG, security]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21656v1)

### Asymmetric Capacity Allocation in Self-Refinement Pipelines

A stage-wise model-size study of self-refinement across Qwen3 and Gemma 3: larger generators and refiners help, refiner size matters most, critic size barely does — but even a small critic beats none. **[inference, efficiency]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21345v1)

## Why It Matters

-   Kagi filtering paywalled results, a felony charge for wiping a phone at the border, and Anna's Archive racing to scan rare books are one story told three ways: who gets to decide which information you can reach, keep, or copy.

-   Tool-unlearning research states the quiet part out loud: a model that "forgot" something hasn't if the agent can retrieve it. Deletion promises now have to cover the whole retrieval stack — weights, tools, and stores — not just the checkpoint.
