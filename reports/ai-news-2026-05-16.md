# The *AI* Briefing

16 May 2026 · Saturday

OpenAI faces a compute wall · PwC bets $300K on Claude · The AI data center rebellion explodes

## Headlines

### OpenAI hits the compute wall

CFO Sarah Friar admits OpenAI may raise more capital despite closing the largest private round in history ($122B). The reason: "a vertical wall of demand" against a compute supply that dries up in 2026. "There is little compute available in 2026," Friar said on Bloomberg TV.

Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-15/openai-may-raise-more-money-as-compute-crunch-deepens-cfo-says) [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/openai-may-raise-more-money-023901571.html)

### PwC bets $300M on Claude across its workforce

PwC expands its alliance with Anthropic: it will roll out Claude Code and Claude Cowork across its entire workforce, starting in the US and scaling to "hundreds of thousands" of employees. Focused on engineering, deals and enterprise operations. The estimated cost is $300M over 5 years.

Sources: [PwC Press Release](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/about-us/newsroom/press-releases/anthropic-pwc-expand-alliance-agentic-enterprise.html) [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/anthropic-pwc-big-four-business-ai-adoption-2026-5)

### ChatGPT becomes your bank

OpenAI launches personal finance in ChatGPT: Pro subscribers can connect bank and investment accounts through Plaid (12,000+ institutions). A dashboard with portfolio, spending, subscriptions and upcoming payments. The reaction has been mostly negative over privacy concerns.

Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/personal-finance-chatgpt/) [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-personal-finance-will-let-you-connect-bank-accounts/)

### The rebellion against AI data centers intensifies

48 data center projects blocked in 2025, worth $156B. In 2026 the resistance is growing: a man threw a Molotov cocktail at Sam Altman's house and threatened to burn down OpenAI's offices (he has since pleaded not guilty). An Indianapolis city councilor was shot 13 times. Public support is collapsing across the board.

Sources: [The Atlantic](https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/2026/05/ai-backlash-data-centers-political-violence/687151/) [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2026/05/08/ai-datacenters-democracy)

### Wall Street revises global AI investment upward

Goldman Sachs raises its consensus estimate for AI capex in 2026 to $527B (from $465B). Gartner projects $2.5T in global AI spending, with $401B in infrastructure alone. Reuters reports that tech companies are turning to the debt markets to finance the expansion, with Nvidia expecting to raise $45-50B in 2026 alone.

Sources: [Goldman Sachs](https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/articles/why-ai-companies-may-invest-more-than-500-billion-in-2026) [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/tech-companies-tap-debt-markets-fund-ai-cloud-expansion-2026-05-11/)

### GPT's 500K agents now in production

OpenAI surpasses the mark of 500,000 GPT agents deployed in production. Most run on AWS infrastructure. The platform lets companies build custom agents for support, sales and operations without writing code.

Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/15/openai-500k-gpt-agents-in-production/) [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/openai-gpt-agents-enterprise/)
