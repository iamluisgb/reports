## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### A Dual-Process Perspective on Nudge Susceptibility in LLM-Based GUI Agents

GUI agents act inside interfaces engineered to steer human judgement, and how much resistance reasoning capability buys against that steering is barely measured. Drawing on dual-process theory, the study separates automatic (Type 1) from reflective (Type 2) digital nudges and runs a randomised online-shopping experiment with 3,600 agents making 21,600 simulated decisions, varying reasoning configuration to isolate what moderates susceptibility. If you deploy computer-use agents, this is the threat model your vendor isn't testing. **[gui-agents, safety, evaluation]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19843v1)

### UnifiedPlayers: Enhance Tool-Integrated Reasoning in Agentic Reinforcement Learning

Self-evolving agent training cuts dependence on human-annotated trajectories, but the standard split — one process generates trajectories, a static verifier judges them — misses failure modes the verifier was never built for, and self-consistency voting entrenches errors shared across trajectories. UnifiedPlayers instead runs three cooperative roles: a Planning Player that generates tasks, an Execution Player that produces multi-turn Python-tool trajectories, and an Evaluation Player that writes executable verifiers, each with role-specific rewards. The hard problem is that every component keeps changing the data and feedback the others train on — the reward design is the proposed answer. **[agents, reinforcement-learning, verifiers]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20089v1)

### Dual-Axis Policy Optimization for LLM Agents

RL for LLM agents contains two optimisation problems that most recipes collapse into one: how environment feedback is attributed to actions within a trajectory, and how complete trajectories are weighted across a batch. BATON attacks both axes — a Bayesian feedback-conditioned posterior over sampled actions, plus Trajectory Mass Normalization giving every complete trajectory equal optimisation mass. On ALFWorld, WebShop, and SearchQA with GRPO and GiGPO, each axis delivers independent gains and the combination is consistently strongest. **[reinforcement-learning, agents, credit-assignment]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19830v1)

### A Unified Evaluation Framework for Trustworthy Large Language Models, Agentic AI, and Multimodal Systems

Benchmark scores are a thin basis for deciding whether a system can be trusted in deployment, and LLMs, agents, and multimodal models each need different evidence. This framework maps output-level, trajectory-level, and cross-modal measurements onto eight trustworthiness dimensions — capability, robustness, safety, fairness, transparency, governance, oversight, efficiency — preserving native metrics while normalising them into common performance bands with uncertainty estimates and traceable evidence. A meta-evaluation layer then audits the evaluation itself for validity, reliability, and reproducibility. **[evaluation, trustworthiness, governance]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19524v1)

### TRACE: Accountable Agentic Retrieval for Source Discovery in Digital Archives

Historical archives break RAG twice: OCR-degraded, genre-heterogeneous text wrecks retrieval, and scholarly use demands provenance a vector store never recorded. TRACE is a training-free agentic retrieval framework for accountable source discovery, built for a project on French Third Republic political discourse and already deployed to 24 researchers across six partner institutions. It is evaluated on HistoriQA-ThirdRepublic — 1,752 French historical questions over digitised parliamentary debates. **[retrieval, provenance, rag]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19897v1)

### Beyond Depth Truncation: Controlled Evaluation of Depth Utilization in Recursive Language Models

The standard test of whether a depth-recurrent model uses its depth — truncate at inference, plot quality against retained depth, read the slope — is broken: truncation simultaneously reduces block applications, reduces distinct computation, and pushes the readout head onto an out-of-distribution residual stream. The convention reads that compound slope as measuring the middle factor alone. This paper's controlled evaluation separates the three, so the slope can finally be evidence about depth utilisation rather than an artefact of the intervention. **[recurrent-models, evaluation, methodology]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19934v1)

## Why It Matters

-   The military's hallucinated report is being read as an argument for keeping analysts upstream of the model — the sharper lesson is that the verifier, not the placement, is the missing component. A fabricated report is precisely the failure mode a review process sized for human-sourced material won't catch, which is UnifiedPlayers' opening premise restated in training-data terms: static verifiers miss emerging failure modes, and self-consistency entrenches errors shared across trajectories. The trust framework's meta-evaluation layer and the depth-truncation critique make the same move from different directions — the thing checking the system needs checking too.

-   The nudge study gives the computer-use cost curve its missing risk line: at a fiftieth of a cent per step, agent fleets will navigate interfaces engineered to manipulate whoever clicks, and 3,600 agents making 21,600 simulated purchases at least turns susceptibility into a measurable quantity. ZCode's silent Git upload and the Second Circuit's border-search ruling are the same fact at other layers — the systems around your data do not share your constraints, and the model's reasoning is not a control. Provenance logging and policy enforcement have to live outside the model, because inside is exactly where the nudges land.

-   Android 17 shipping APIs with no AOSP release and Claude Code adopting the vendor-neutral AGENTS.md point in opposite directions, and together they confirm that openness is now a per-layer property rather than a product attribute. The papers supply the toolkit for the unauditable half: TRACE anchors every retrieval to the parent-case trajectory at the matched state, and the trust framework demands traceable evidence behind every score. Expect provenance to arrive as a procurement requirement long before it becomes a research fashion.
