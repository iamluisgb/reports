Why It Matters
▸ The Nvidia alliance without OpenAI/Google/Anthropic is a power move: it defines "AI security" around open-weight models that defenders can inspect. If the closed-model companies don't join, they're implicitly positioned as the security risk. This is the opening salvo in the open vs closed wars.
▸ AI doubling vulnerability discovery is a double-edged sword: more flaws found means more attack surface exposed, but also more flaws fixed. The real risk is the patch gap — organizations that can't remediate at AI speed will have a growing backlog of exploitable vulnerabilities. This is where AIOps pays for itself.
▸ Anthropic's position is nuanced: pro-open-weights but anti-distillation-at-scale. The $500 fine-tune story complicates their argument — if a 9B model can beat frontier on specific tasks for pocket change, the "distillation harms open competition" narrative weakens. The real question is whether task-specific fine-tuning is fundamentally different from model distillation.
