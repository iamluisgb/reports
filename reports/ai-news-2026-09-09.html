Think Before You Link: Rarity, Reasoning, and Retrieval in Multilingual Entity Linking
Knowledge-graph structural metrics expose rare entities that pageview measures miss; a training-free reason-and-retrieve VLM lifts MERLIN accuracy 6.9% overall and up to 23.3% on rare-entity slices. [retrieval, entity-linking, multilingual]

03
When Synthetic Data Hurts: On Catastrophic Forgetting in Skill Retrieval for LLM Agents
A production skill router over 34,396 skills shows synthetic-data fine-tuning improves in-distribution retrieval but causes catastrophic forgetting on real and OOD data; continual-learning regularizers recover it and add 13.98% for a 0.6B Qwen retriever. [agents, retrieval, fine-tuning]

DriftNet: A Dual-Head Trajectory Transformer for Detecting and Localizing Prompt Injection in LLM Agents
A sub-two-million-parameter model flags compromised agent trajectories, pins the injection point, and labels hijacked steps in one pass, reaching 0.983 trajectory-level F1 on AgentDrift while keeping false alarms low. [agents, security, prompt-injection]
