## Why It Matters

-   Apple's pivot from iterating M6 to launching AI-native M7 silicon confirms that edge inference, not general-purpose compute, is now the primary design constraint for consumer hardware. For SRE teams, this means AI workloads continue migrating from cloud inference toward device-local — rethinking latency, privacy, and cache hierarchies.

-   Framework's 10G Ethernet module teardown reveals that PCIe tunneling and USB-C alt-mode negotiation are still unresolved UX problems for high-speed peripherals. As AI agents demand more local bandwidth, SREs should expect these bottlenecks to surface in edge deployments and rack interconnects.
