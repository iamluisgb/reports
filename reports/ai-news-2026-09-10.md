## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### MindTopo: Can Foundation Models Reason in Topological Space?

MindTopo benchmarks 14 multimodal LLMs on five topological properties — continuity, separation, order, enclosure and knots — across 11,030 reasoning and closed-loop planning instances. Every model scores higher on reasoning than on planning, and the best still falls well short of human performance. **[reasoning, benchmarks, multimodal]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11900v1)

### Domain-Specific Hallucination Detection in Large Language Models

A pipeline combining a fine-tuned DeBERTa-v3 classifier, MC Dropout uncertainty and temperature scaling reaches F1=0.915 and AUROC=0.977 on HaluEval, then uses DPO to cut a Qwen2.5-0.5B generator's hallucination rate from 85.5% to 37.7%. Transfer to the biomedical SciFact benchmark drops to F1=0.52, motivating domain-specific fine-tuning. **[hallucination, eval, fine-tuning]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11878v1)

### RetroThinker: Enabling Retrospective Thinking in Speech LLMs

RetroThinker post-trains the streaming Moshi speech model to self-verify and correct its chain-of-thought during inference, using curated retrospective data plus length-based DPO. On GSM8K it gains 11% absolute accuracy at comparable latency. **[speech, reasoning, inference]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11864v1)

### RAG-Safety-Bench: Reliable Evaluation of Retrieval-Augmented LLM Safety

The benchmark isolates how retrieval changes LLM safety across four conditions — no RAG, an oracle harmful document, related documents without the answer, and random safe documents — on five open-source models. It finds an inverse relationship between benign and unsafe capability, and that baseline guardrails give no downstream safety guarantee once retrieval is enabled. **[RAG, safety, eval]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11758v1)

### Nuha-Speech: Building General-Purpose Arabic Speech-LLMs

Nuha-Speech builds an Arabic speech question-answering corpus of over 1.5 million training samples, fine-tunes Qwen-Omni variants at several scales, and defines a task suite with tailored metrics for evaluating Arabic speech-LLMs. **[speech, multilingual, datasets]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11892v1)

-   Model launches dominate the top of HN, but the stories drawing the most comments are about trust: unpublished math handed to OpenAI, training-data claims, and a consent toggle that re-enables itself. The argument has moved from capability to attribution and consent.
