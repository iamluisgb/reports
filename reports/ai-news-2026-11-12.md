## Papers — ArXiv

### Bits or permutations? Spending a fixed inference budget on weight precision or on option-order averaging in small local language models

Local models can store weights in fewer bits and average answer-option reorderings to reduce order sensitivity; this paper asks whether quantization worsens that sensitivity and which spend is cheaper under a fixed inference budget. On Qwen3-0.6B and Qwen3-1.7B with simulated RTN at 8/6/4/3 bits and 300 items each on ARC-Easy, ARC-Challenge, MMLU and HellaSwag, 6 bits loses no accuracy (+1.8 and +0.9 points, unexplained), 8 bits changes it by -0.7 and -0.4 points, and 4 bits raises answer-slot skew from 0.15 to 0.53 on 0.6B and 0.07 to 0.12 on 1.7B. **[quantization, small models, evaluation]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14413v1)

### SafeRoute-MAS: Coverage-Aware and Risk-Calibrated Message Routing for Multi-Agent Scientific Reasoning

Multi-agent scientific committees accumulate specialist messages, and routing must remove tokens without dropping the coverage that integration needs. SafeRoute-MAS reports +1.80 pp (95% CI +1.13 to +2.52) on a sealed cache of 5,328 committees while removing 12.76% of message tokens, but a controlled 4,580-integration run over a 458-committee sample gives -0.09 points, +0.94 points after design weighting (CI -1.31 to +3.50), and no token-matched selector advantage (+1.68, CI -1.27 to +5.10). Only 13 of 88 cached non-neutral labels retain direction by repeated-call majority—the routing win is workload-specific. **[multi-agent, routing, evaluation]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13454v1)

### Evaluating LLM-Generated World Models for Neuro-Symbolic Reasoning

Formal world models support planning and verification, but generating them from open-ended multi-agent descriptions is hard and mechanical validity may not mean the model represents the intended decision problem. The benchmark covers 44 scenarios and five LLMs, with 410 first-pass generations; in the reference-backed game track 134/135 pass a strict mechanical audit, yet structural and semantic checks still separate plausible from faithful models. **[world models, neuro-symbolic, benchmark]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14419v1)

### SteerDistill: Quantizing LLM Agents via Closed-Loop Teacher Steering

Low-bit quantization cuts agent inference cost but can break task success because quantized rollouts overlook requirements and environmental conditions, producing transitions that diverge from full-precision references. SteerDistill has a full-precision teacher revise failed responses, iteratively refines that guidance from student execution, then trains the student without guidance on the locally verified corrections. The abstract gives no headline success delta—treat the method as promising, not proven, until the full results table. **[agent quantization, distillation, evaluation]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13410v1)

## Why It Matters

-   The agent stack is moving from capability demos to control surfaces. SafeRoute-MAS finds a token-removal win on a sealed cache (+1.80 pp, 12.76% fewer message tokens) but cannot reproduce a selector advantage under controlled integration, while the world-model benchmark shows 134/135 mechanically valid models can still miss the intended decision problem. United banning AI bookings and Google shipping a Gemini Decisions API point the same way: the product is now the boundary, policy and verification layer, not the model call.

-   Cost work is getting more honest about where losses appear. The bits-versus-permutations study finds 6-bit weights lose no accuracy on its setup, but 4 bits sharply raises answer-slot skew (0.15→0.53 on Qwen3-0.6B), while SteerDistill's abstract offers a correction loop without a headline delta; Apple's retrieval work and AWS's local emulator attack the same economics from the token and cloud-round-trip ends. The reported Nvidia board move against Jensen Huang lands in the middle of that repricing—the GPU economy's centre is no longer a fixed assumption.

-   Engineering discipline remains the differentiator. Tim Peters's death is a reminder that the invisible primitives—Timsort, the Zen—outlast product cycles; the Apache httpd RCE and GitHub Actions pinning guide are the same lesson in security form. Jane Street's near-miss and Atuin's agent-session sync show operations and observability are being rebuilt for a world where agents, not humans, generate most terminal and cloud actions.
