## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### Agent Memory with Episodic Retrieval for Financial Decision-Making

Introduces META, a multi-agent trading framework that pairs indicator agents with an episodic memory module storing past trading episodes as embeddings, reweighting signals under similar market regimes to improve directional accuracy. **[agents, RAG, finance]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28771v1)

### Benchmarking Argumentative Behaviour of LLMs: A Study of Defences Against Character Attacks

Contrasts LLM dialogues with U.S. presidential debate corpora on ad hominem exchanges, finding safety fine-tuning pushes models toward logical defences while forgoing the ethotic counterattacks human debaters deploy strategically. **[eval, safety]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28673v1)

### Agent-Editing World Model: Rethinking World Modeling for LLM Agents

Proposes a world model that edits agent task states instead of simulating tool responses, combining an Action Judge with State Revision to clean contaminated reasoning histories, adding 3.2–6.7 points across six agent benchmarks. **[agents, world-models]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28416v1)

### Mizar: A 159M-Parameter Audio-Language Model for Audio Understanding

Details a 159M-parameter audio-language model pairing a compact audio encoder with SmolLM2-135M, beating prior sub-200M models on MMAU, MMAR and ADQA while answering questions on a single CPU in about a second. **[audio, small-models, edge]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28344v1)

### Learning the Cost of Reliable Inference

Designs a reverse-auction procurement platform where LLM providers bid per task and queries route to the cheapest provider meeting a quality threshold, estimating fixed per-token pricing leaves margins of 10–71% on the table. **[inference, routing, pricing]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28322v2)
