Defending Wearable VLMs Against Private Attribute Inference
TGAP learns a residual transformation of visual tokens before they leave the trusted device boundary, cutting private-attribute accuracy from 56.7% to 7.4% on a 3,221-record privacy-utility benchmark while holding relaxed utility at 74.4%. [privacy] [VLM] [inference]

FaithSieve: Fine-Grained Evaluation of Math Proofs with Faithful Formal Evidence
Decomposes natural-language proofs into local reasoning units and gates Lean evidence on semantic alignment, reaching 81.4% exact first-error accuracy on a 350-problem Olympiad set versus 72.3% for direct judging. [reasoning] [eval] [formal-methods]

VBVR-Pro: A Scalable and Verifiable Suite for Native Visual Reasoning
A 300-task procedurally generated testbed with rule-grounded reward scorers; across 30+ generators, video holds up best when tasks require persistent spatiotemporal state tracking. [visual-reasoning] [RL] [benchmarks]
