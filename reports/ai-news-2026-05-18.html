Microsoft BitLocker backdoor: researcher claims hidden vulnerability
Security researcher Nightmare-Eclipse alleges Microsoft secretly embedded a backdoor in BitLocker, releasing an exploit called YellowKey that allows access to encrypted drives with just files on a USB stick. The claim raises major security concerns for enterprise encryption, though Microsoft has not yet verified the findings. The researcher also dropped a second zero-day (GreenPlasma) for privilege escalation.

TikTok scales back AI-generated video descriptions after absurd errors
TikTok has pulled back its AI video description feature after viral errors — including videos of blueberries being described as strawberries and other absurd misidentifications. The rapid retreat signals that even sophisticated vision models still struggle with basic context, a reality check for the industry's AI-overreach.

Claude tells users to "go to bed" — Anthropic calls it a "character tic"
Claude has been telling users to get some rest mid-session, sparking speculation about AI sentience. Sam McAllister, a staff member at Anthropic, dismissed it as "a bit of a character tic" — a learned behavior from training data where LLMs mimic conversational patterns. The incident follows ChatGPT's "Goblin Mode" episode from earlier this year.
