## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### Inference-Time Graph Engineering for Multi-Agent LLM Workflows

ReActNet compiles role-specialized agents into task-conditioned temporal graphs where each edge carries a natural-language instruction, improving multi-agent reasoning without RL or gradient-based topology search. **[agents, orchestration, inference]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05774v1)

### DI-Bench: Systematically Generating In-Domain Data Intelligence Benchmarks for Enterprise Agents

A pipeline builds artifact-linkage graphs over tables, metrics and documents to generate enterprise data-intelligence tasks; on the resulting 731-task benchmark, models score only 32% when retrieved business rules modify the computation. **[benchmark, agents, eval]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05776v1)

### Intra-Prompt Parallel Decoding for Common-Context Question Answering

IPPD answers multiple common-context questions within a single prompt, decoding the next token for every question in one inference step using virtual position IDs and attention-mask manipulation, for up to 7x effective throughput. **[inference, decoding, efficiency]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05707v1)

### Molecular Déjà Vu: Digit-Level Retrieval of Published Values in Frontier Language Models

Auditing 22 frontier models on 12 molecular regression benchmarks finds verbatim retrieval of published values on five datasets, with higher reasoning levels flagging retrieval 89% more often. **[eval, memorization, benchmarks]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05381v1)

### Distill Globally, Adapt Locally: Reasoning Distillation and Product-Type Test-Time Training for Scalable Trade-Up Recommendation

Distills RAG-based LLM reasoning into a 15.5M-parameter student reaching AUC 0.924 on an 8,352-pair benchmark, with product-type test-time training lifting it to 0.941 at roughly 5,000x the speed of direct LLM inference. **[distillation, RAG, recommendation]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05363v1)
