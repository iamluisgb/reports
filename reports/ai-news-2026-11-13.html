01
AgentTorrent: A Dataset of Context and Harness Engineering Artifacts on GitHub
Agentic coding has quietly shifted from writing prompts to committing harness configuration — skills, hooks, MCP configs, specs and plans — into repositories. AgentTorrent captures that layer at scale: 62,374 configuration artifacts, 27,593 planning documents and 1.2 million AI-credited commits across 10,944 projects covering six agentic tools. First large empirical record of what developers actually engineer around the model, with all collection heuristics released. [datasets, harness-engineering, coding-agents]

02
Repository-Context-Guided Image Understanding for Agentic Software Development
Coding agents receive UI screenshots in bug reports, but low resolution, compression and small rendered text degrade vision-language model reliability precisely where localisation matters. The fix is to render reference screenshots from the same repository the agent is modifying, giving it crisp visual evidence alongside the buggy state. The degraded-input problem becomes a context-engineering problem, solvable with tooling rather than a bigger VLM. [coding-agents, multimodal, bug-localisation]

03
Joint Quantization and Dynamic Partitioning for Context-Aware Edge Vision Transformers
Static split-inference fixes the partition point and cannot track time-varying bandwidth or load in vehicle-to-vehicle settings. This work formulates partition point, per-layer quantisation bitwidth and placement as a single MILP minimising compute plus communication energy under latency and quality constraints, evaluated across three Jetson Orin tiers on OPV2V multi-stream traffic. Joint optimisation beats adapting one knob at a time when both compute and link quality move. [edge-inference, quantisation, orchestration]

04
Pixie: A Context-Aware Multi-Agent Multi-Modal LLM Architecture for Digital Marketing
Single-agent prompting plateaus on domain workflows where accuracy and consistency both matter. Pixie pairs an orchestrator with task-specific and reconciliation agents, mixing zero-shot prompting with proprietary strategy guidelines and statistical-model documentation; expert assessments and output-variance measurements show the multi-agent setup improving decision quality in marketing tasks. Another datapoint that reviewer-agent patterns are becoming the default production architecture. [multi-agent, orchestration, evaluation]

Why It Matters
▸ The harness is now a dependency with a maintenance contract — and nobody is honouring it. AgentTorrent shows developers committing tens of thousands of hooks, skills and MCP configs into production repos; Anthropic deleting a Claude Code feature with no deprecation window shows what happens when that layer is owned by someone else's release cadence. AgentTorrent gives you the data to see how much of your stack is now prompt-adjacent configuration; the Claude Code issue is what it looks like when that configuration silently breaks.
▸ Orchestration has become the product surface. Gemini 4 Hydrogen ships with an Orchestrator component, Pixie's numbers support orchestrator-plus-reviewer topologies over single-agent prompting, and the edge ViT paper is orchestration too — jointly solving partition, quantisation and placement under moving constraints. The frontier of applied work is no longer the model but the policy that routes around it, which is also why Microsoft 365's multi-hour degradation matters: your orchestrator inherits every upstream failure.
▸ Local and edge inference is consolidating from hacks into real options, and the constraint is trust, not capability. Apple's Foundation Models framework, smolmaxxing and Edge0-145B's local-hardware claims all push per-token billing off the invoice — benchmark before believing the frontier claim. The checkpoint-forensics work is the sharper point: hardware signatures persist in published weights, so where and on what a model was trained is increasingly auditable. Provenance is becoming a property you must assume leaks, whether or not you have anything to hide.
