## Headlines

### GPT-5.6 closes 30-year convex optimization gap with single prompt

Following a US export-control directive that forced a global suspension on June 12, Anthropic redeployed Claude Fable 5 on July 1 with updated cybersecurity safeguards. The Series H valued Anthropic at $965B. Claude Sonnet 5 also launched June 30.

US, Australia, Canada, UK, and NZ jointly published "Careful Adoption of Agentic AI Services" — a security framework for deploying agentic AI in critical infrastructure and defense environments. The first coordinated intergovernmental guidance on agent risks.

## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### LongStraw: Long-Context RL Beyond 2M Tokens under a Fixed GPU Budget

Bridges the growing gap between inference (million-token contexts) and RL post-training (256K tokens). Proposes a training recipe that extends effective RL context length beyond 2M tokens without additional GPU budget. **[RL · long-context]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14952)

### SEED: Self-Evolving On-Policy Distillation for Agentic Reinforcement Learning

LLMs trained as interactive agents for long-horizon multi-turn tasks. Uses outcome-based RL with on-policy distillation where the model learns from its own rollouts, creating a self-improving loop for agentic capabilities. **[agents · RL]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14777)

### SearchOS-V1: Towards Robust Open-Domain Information-Seeking Agent Collaboration

As tool-integrated LLMs make web search a core agent capability, long interaction histories cause agents to lose task tracking. Proposes a collaborative multi-agent search architecture that maintains robust state across long-running information-seeking sessions. **[agents · search]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15257)

### Smarter and Cheaper at Once: Byte-Exact KV-Cache Grafting

Freeze a small model and make it both more capable and cheaper — no weight changes. Verified knowledge is deposited as a byte-exact KV state artifact and grafted at inference. A frozen small model becomes a verified-knowledge flywheel. **[inference · efficiency]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14431)

### BadWAM: When World-Action Models Dream Right but Act Wrong

World-action models (WAMs) for embodied control learn representations coupling action generation with future world prediction. Shows a fundamental failure mode: WAMs can accurately predict world dynamics yet still produce incorrect actions, challenging the assumption that world modeling is sufficient for control. **[embodied · RL]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15207)

### Partition, Prompt, Aggregate: Statistical Self-Consistency in Language Models

If in-context learning is a form of conditional inference, then identical prompts should yield consistent conditionals. Proposes a statistical framework (partition-prompt-aggregate) to measure and improve self-consistency, with implications for reliable agent behavior. **[LLM · reliability]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15277)

### VideoChat3: Fully Open Video MLLM for Efficient and Generalist Video Understanding

Open-source video MLLM addressing motion, long-video, and streaming interaction. Bridges the gap between closed-source video models and fully open alternatives with efficient architectures for real-world video understanding. **[multimodal · video]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14935)
