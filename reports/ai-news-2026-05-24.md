## Why It Matters

-   Microsoft's AI cost data is the canary in the coal mine: the unit economics of agent deployments define whether AI replaces or supplements human work. For SREs, this means token efficiency, caching strategies, and model quantization aren't optimization — they're existential to the business case.

-   Code with Claude and the explosion of agent benchmarks (TerminalWorld, ACC, HarnessAPI) signal that agentic infrastructure is maturing faster than most enterprises expect. The question is no longer "will agents write code" but "who builds the runtime, monitoring, and security layer around them."

-   The gap between "compile agentic workflows into weights" (arXiv 2605.22502) and traditional orchestration (LangGraph, CrewAI) suggests a fundamental architectural shift coming — from external orchestrators to fused agent models. The next 12 months will determine which approach wins.
