## Why It Matters

-   Kimi K3 weights are live. The largest open-weight model in history is now downloadable by anyone. This is the moment where US export controls either prove effective (if the model can't run without restricted hardware) or prove irrelevant (if it runs on consumer GPUs). The answer changes the geopolitics of AI.

-   Dell+NVIDIA bringing trillion-parameter inference to the desktop signals that the enterprise AI stack is moving on-prem. Combined with Kimi K3's open weights, the infrastructure for running frontier AI locally has never been more accessible. The cloud-first assumption in AI is cracking.

-   Two papers — Skill Self-Play and Regression Tax — tackle the same fundamental question from opposite angles: how do you make LLM agents better without breaking what already works? This is the quality engineering problem that will define the next generation of agent frameworks.
