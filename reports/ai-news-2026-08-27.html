Pairs a frozen CLIP ViT-L/14 with Qwen2.5-7B through image and region bridges and a zero-shot detector stack, reaching 82.85% mean F1 on POPE against 81.14% for an image-tokens-only baseline. [multimodal, grounding, hallucination]

If Agents Were Angels, No Governance Would Be Necessary: Out-of-Band Policy Enforcement at a Trusted Tool Boundary
Moves authorization outside agent reasoning, narrowing queries and filtering or masking responses; across 3,621 trials trace failures drop from 57.6% to 0.2% while fulfilment falls from 79.1% to 60.9%. [agents, security, policy]

LongGuard: Mechanistic Analysis and Training-Free Mitigation of Long-Context Failure in Safety Guardrails
SafetyNIAH shows unsafe recall in 15 guardrails drops by more than 50% across a 0.25k-32k context grid, tracing it to diluted attention on the unsafe needle, then recovers 22% and 13% on a six-guardrail average with two training-free mitigations. [guardrails, long-context, safety]

Why It Matters
▸ If Nvidia owns Hugging Face, the default distribution channel for open-weight models sits inside the largest accelerator vendor. The neutrality that made the hub worth publishing to becomes a policy question, not a technical one — and it lands the same week "Small Models Have Arrived" argues the frontier is no longer where the value is.
▸ Today's papers point the same direction: the hard part is no longer capability but containment. LongGuard shows guardrails silently degrade with context length, and the out-of-band policy work shows what it takes to hold a boundary outside the model's own reasoning — 41 points of trace-failure reduction bought with 18 points of fulfilment.
