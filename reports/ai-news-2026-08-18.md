## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### The Lifecycle of LLM-as-a-Judge for Large-Scale Recommendation Explanations

Netflix lays out the full lifecycle of the LLM judges that grade recommendation explanations — birth, training, deployment and drift monitoring — validated in a five-week A/B test across tens of millions of members. **[eval, LLM-judge, production]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18300v3)

### Rigorous Evaluation of Large Language Models for Malaria Drug Discovery

The Malaria-Instruct benchmark shows fine-tuned open-source LLMs (TxGemma-9B, ROC-AUC 0.731) beating frontier models like Gemini 2.5 and o3 on out-of-distribution malaria virtual screening. **[fine-tuning, drug-discovery, eval]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20418v1)

### A Comprehensive Review of Large Language Models for Nanophotonics

A survey of LLMs in nanophotonics, from surrogate models that cast structure–spectrum mapping as a language task to agentic systems that orchestrate simulations for closed-loop design optimization. **[review, agents, simulation]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18279v2)

### TokEval: A Tokenizer Evaluation Suite

A tokenizer evaluation framework that goes beyond fertility and compression rate with metrics — UTF-8 boundary integrity, digit place-value alignment — shown to predict downstream language, math and code performance. **[tokenizers, eval, pretraining]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18062v3)

### ComponentBench: Diagnosing Component-Level Failures in Computer-Use Agents

2,910 tasks over 97 canonical UI components stress-test computer-use agents: the same model swings 30%+ in success rate depending only on observation/action space, and the fastest setup still runs 3.7× slower than a human reference. **[agents, benchmark, GUI]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18307v1)
