### The Big Three IPO Race: SpaceX, OpenAI, and Anthropic

The artificial intelligence landscape is shifting from private giants to public markets. SpaceX, OpenAI, and Anthropic are all finalizing plans to debut on stock exchanges, testing investor appetite for the infrastructure and intelligence layers of the new economy.

### Google's Massive Bet on SpaceX Compute

Google has inked a staggering deal to pay SpaceX $920 million per month for access to specialized compute infrastructure. The agreement, running from late 2026 through 2029, highlights the desperate scramble for reliable, high-scale processing capacity.

Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/05/google-will-pay-spacex-920m-per-month-for-compute/)

The focus on "embodied AI" is accelerating. Neura Robotics' latest funding round marks a significant step in bringing sophisticated AI models into physical, robotic hardware at scale.
