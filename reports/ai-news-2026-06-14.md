## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### SpatialClaw: Rethinking Action Interface for Agentic Spatial Reasoning

A novel action interface for spatial reasoning in AI agents, treating 3D interaction as a learned skill rather than a pipeline of perception-planning-execution. Achieves SOTA on EmbodiedQA.

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13673) **[Jun 2026]**

### Learning to Reason by Analogy via Retrieval-Augmented Reinforcement Finetuning

Combines RAG with RL finetuning to teach LLMs analogical reasoning. The model retrieves structurally-similar problems and learns to map solutions across domains. Strong gains on GSM8K and MATH.

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13680) **[Jun 2026]**

### Influcoder: Distilling Decoders’ Gradient Influence Rankings into an Encoder

Distills gradient-based data influence from a large decoder into an efficient encoder for data curation at scale. Enables training data quality filtering without expensive backward passes.

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13668) **[Jun 2026]**

### HyperTool: Beyond Step-Wise Tool Calls for Tool-Augmented Agents

Introduces hyper-tool calls that batch multiple tool invocations into a single planning step, reducing latency by 40% in agentic workflows. Tested on GAIA and ToolBench.

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13663) **[Jun 2026]**

### Recursive Agent Harnesses Recursive LM Calls for Long-Context Reasoning

Shows that structured recursion over model calls outperforms monolithic long-context processing on multi-hop reasoning benchmarks. A practical alternative to context window scaling.

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13643) **[Jun 2026]**

### Beyond the Commitment Boundary: Probing Epiphenomenal Chain-of-Thought

Investigates whether CoT reasoning is causal or epiphenomenal via intervention experiments. Finds that human-interpretable CoT steps are often post-hoc rationalizations, not actual reasoning traces.

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13603) **[Jun 2026]**
