## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### DeepSAGE: Stage-Aware Reinforcement Learning for Structured CBT Counseling Dialogue

An LLM plus deep-RL framework that models a first CBT session as eleven stages: an external controller decides stage completion while a learned policy picks the therapeutic intention that shapes the response. Beats six retrieval, prompting, stage and policy baselines on simulated client engagement, though the authors note it measures dialogue control, not clinical effectiveness. **[counseling, reinforcement-learning, dialogue]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22615v1)

### Learning Generalizable Behaviors for Terminal Agents

Argues RL mainly shapes high-level routing behaviors on top of pre-trained skills, so reward quality matters more than environment count. Their River recipe filters low-quality environments and adds process-level regularization, improving RL gains by 106% and 30% on Terminal-Bench-Lite and Terminal-Bench-v2.1 using under 30% of the training environments. **[agents, reinforcement-learning, terminal]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22631v2)

### Teaching LLMs How ICU Physicians Approach Clinical Reasoning Through OMOP-Aligned Retrieval Improves Reasoning Across Clinical Domains

ICU-REACT is a reasoning dataset built with 19 clinicians so models learn retrieval and context-aware reasoning in intensive care. Clin-REACT models from 8B to 70B across three families beat their backbones and other open medical LLMs on five clinical reasoning benchmarks, with gains transferring to script concordance and downstream diagnosis tasks. **[clinical, RAG, reasoning]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22622v1)

### Coalition-Aware Skill Reliability for Self-Evolving Agents

Audits skill banks in self-evolving agents and finds two recurring failures: coalition pollution, where bank-level gains hide skills that hurt in combination, and cross-domain utility reversal after transfer. Proposes Shapley-marginal skill selection (CASS) and a label-free masking optimizer (u-SMCO), which improve results on LoCoMo, LongMemEval, HotpotQA and ALFWorld. **[agents, skills, eval]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22610v1)

### GTA-RAG: Graph-Trajectory-Augmented Reinforcement Learning for Multi-Turn Retrieval-Augmented Reasoning

Samples connected document paths from an entity-document graph to synthesize multi-hop trajectories, validates them against the deployed retriever, then trains the retrieval policy with GRPO and a trajectory-guided reward. Outperforms RL-based RAG baselines on Qwen2.5-3B and 7B backbones with substantially better evidence-chain coverage. **[RAG, agents, reinforcement-learning]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22479v2)
