## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### BioEVAL: A global, multi-institutional benchmark of large language and multimodal models for bioengineering

A benchmark for bioengineering built by 22 research groups: 608 PhD-level items across 11 subfields, spanning multiple-choice questions, literature synthesis and experimental image interpretation. Frontier models reach up to 90% accuracy on MCQs but vary substantially across subfields, mapping where biomedical AI capability actually stands. **[evaluation, biomedical, multimodal]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30489v1)

### The Hard Part Comes After Search: Benchmarking Web Agents on Synthesizing, Organizing, and Displaying Knowledge

Computer-use benchmarks stop at retrieval; useful assistants have to synthesize what they find into a finished artifact. KNOWS runs open-ended browser tasks that end in a document, deck or spreadsheet, each paired with an evaluator combining deterministic checks and LLM judgments. The best frontier agent fully completes fewer than 3% of tasks, and failures on visual steps alone can make the artifact unusable. **[agents, computer-use, benchmarks]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30205v2)

### Reinforcement Learning of Communication in a Mesh of Small Language Models

Majority voting saturates once extra samples echo the same answer, so TalkMesh trains small LM agents to communicate: the most confident agent broadcasts a hint, the rest revise, and gossip consensus approximates a confidence-weighted vote without a coordinator. Three agents with six total outputs match 32-sample self-consistency, and a defended mesh keeps 0.507 accuracy when four of eight agents collude with poisoned hints. **[multi-agent, inference, robustness]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30604v1)

### T-RoPE: Time-Aware Rotary Position Embedding for Sequential Recommendation

Recommenders inherited RoPE from language models, where position encodes token order and nothing about real time. T-RoPE rotates by timestamps instead, with multiscale frequency banks and non-stationary keys, breaking translation invariance so seasonal context becomes distinguishable. It tops every metric on five public benchmarks, gains 13–82% over an industrial backbone on a 6B-interaction dataset, and ships with a positive online A/B test. **[recommendation, transformers, temporal]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30578v1)

### Feeding BabyLMs Macaroni: Code-Switching Curricula Cause Cross-Lingual Convergence

Children in multilingual communities code-switch mid-sentence; can pretraining data do the same? Mixing word- and sentence-level code-switching into 100M-word multilingual corpora aligns the representations of parallel text across scripts, and a curriculum that progresses from word-level switching to monolingual text beats plain-mixing baselines on the BabyLM evaluation suite. **[pretraining, multilingual, data]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30576v1)

### Do LLMs Understand Context? A Knowledge Graph-Based Evaluation Framework

Perplexity and BLEU grade surface performance, not whether a model grounds its answers in the context it was given. This framework scores QA by comparing knowledge graphs extracted from context and response — S3KG blends structural and semantic similarity into one measure — and adds triplet-level diagnostics that categorize where reasoning fails. Up to +7.6 F1 over the strongest baseline across nine benchmarks, with AUROC up to 0.973. **[evaluation, knowledge-graphs, QA]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30535v1)

### Where Does Retrieval-Based Open-Ended Evaluation Fail? Automatic Taxonomy Induction from Long-Form Medical Answer Factuality Verification

Retrieve-then-verify is the standard answer to hallucination in medical text, but aggregate F1 hides where it breaks. Two taxonomies — five retrieval-stage quality dimensions and six verifier-reasoning steps — are induced automatically and stress-tested across 4 retrieval methods and 6 frontier verifiers. Bigger models, more reasoning effort, authoritative web sources and medical fine-tuning all fail to fix the failure modes: the limits look like the paradigm's, not the implementation's. **[RAG, hallucination, medical]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30484v1)
