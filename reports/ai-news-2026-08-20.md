## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### AEGIS: Preventing Cross-Domain Resource Abuse in MCP

Targets MCP tools that let agents request outsized resources — huge search radii, long videos, heavy image jobs — and proposes policy enforcement that generalizes across text, image, video and location modalities. **[MCP, agents, security]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20481v1)

### MidTool: Mid-training Data Synthesis for Agentic Tool Use

An open pipeline that mixes web, PDF and code data with supervision synthesized from real tool APIs, MCP skills and document-grounded workflows, aimed at teaching affordance recognition, argument grounding and workflow composition. **[tool use, mid-training, data]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20314v1)

### When Failures Propagate: Causal Failure Attribution in Agentic RAG

AgenticRAG-FP injects a certified fault at a chosen hop, re-executes the downstream trajectory, and tests whether post-hoc diagnosis still finds the injected hop; coverage-based diagnosis reports 0.91 coverage at hop 1 and 0.00 at hops 2 and 3. **[RAG, eval, failure attribution]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20627v1)

### Evaluating Skills, Not Just Agents: Agentic Continuous Evaluation of Skills

ACES runs paired live trials with and without a candidate skill, normalizes trajectories into ATIF, and reports Skill Lift as the skill's added value under a fixed task, harness, workspace and scorer. **[agents, eval, skills]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20614v1)

### Pandora's AI Model Routing Box: Efficient Allocation with Costly Value Estimation

Casts routing across specialists as optimal search with costly inspection, and derives closed-form value-of-information rules for deciding when a better estimate of a model's expected return is worth its cost. **[routing, inference, cost]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20316v2)

## Why It Matters

-   The top of the day is not a model release: scraping at Meta's scale, AI text pushed into other people's reading, and fingerprinting that runs without consent. The contested ground has moved from what models can do to what is acceptable to do with automated collection.

-   The ArXiv list is dominated by control rather than capability — rate-limiting MCP tools, attributing failures in multi-hop RAG, and measuring whether a skill actually lifts an agent's score. Evaluation and enforcement are becoming the load-bearing engineering work.
