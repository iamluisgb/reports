## Why It Matters

-   Bonsai 27B running on-device + Claude Science + Gwern's Guardian Angels — the AI stack is fracturing into edge, scientific, and personal-agent tiers. The era of "one model to rule them all" is giving way to specialized, distributed intelligence. For SREs, this means heterogeneous inference infrastructure, not monoculture.

-   Tailscale SSH vuln + Microsoft's record 570 patches + Five Eyes agentic AI guidance — the security surface area is expanding faster than patch cycles can keep up. Agentic systems introduce a whole new class of supply chain and trust-boundary vulnerabilities that current runbooks don't cover.

-   "The Tower Keeps Rising" and multi-agent LLM coordination failures — we're building agents on top of architectures whose complexity is compounding. Every new abstraction layer we add (RAG, MCP, agent orchestrators) must be interrogated for whether it reduces or obscures the cognitive load on the operator.
