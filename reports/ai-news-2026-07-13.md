## Why It Matters

-   **Agent efficiency is the new battleground.** GPT-5.6 (2.2× faster, 27% cheaper), Claude Code's 50% limit boost, and OpenCode's 7k vs 33k token overhead all signal a market shift from raw capability to cost-per-task. The agent framework you choose in 2026 will be judged on token economics, not just reasoning quality.

-   **AI safety regulation is hardening.** Five Eyes agentic AI guidance + Illinois SB 315 + Anthropic/OpenAI backing third-party audits — 2026 is the year AI governance shifts from voluntary to structural. Production agents need audit trails and human-in-the-loop baked in from day one.

-   **GhostLock in Linux for 15 years.** Foundational infra security is still catching up with AI deployment speed. Every agent running on Linux inherits the kernel's attack surface — and we're still finding 15-year-old bugs. Defense in depth isn't optional.
