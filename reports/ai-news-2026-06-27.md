## 📄 Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### The Unfireable Safety Kernel: Execution-Time AI Alignment for AI Agents

Proposes placing alignment controls outside the agent's address space — system prompts and guardrails inside the runtime are escapable. An execution-time kernel that agents cannot bypass. **[safety, alignment, agents]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26057)

### Uncertainty Quantification for Computer-Use Agents

Benchmarks post-hoc uncertainty quantification for VLM-based GUI agents — critical for rejection, calibration, and spatial safety regions when agents click through interfaces. **[UQ, VLM, agents]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25760)

### Heuresis: Search Strategies for Autonomous AI Research Agents

LLM-based agents need to explore ideas that are simultaneously performant, diverse, and novel. Introduces search strategies across quality, diversity, and novelty dimensions. **[research agents, exploration]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25198)

### Neglected Free Lunch from Post-training: Progress Advantage for LLM Agents

Process reward models enable step-level evaluation of LLMs, but building them for agentic settings is prohibitively difficult — long-horizon interactions, irreversible actions, stochastic feedback. **[PRM, agents, training]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26080)

### Decoupling Reconnaissance and Exploitation in LLM Pen Testing

End-to-end black-box evaluations of LLM pen testing agents are susceptible to error cascading — failures in early recon mask actual exploitation ability. Proposes decoupled evaluation. **[security, LLM, evaluation]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25332)

### ASAP: Agent-System Co-Design for Auto HPO Research

Every HPO tool relies on a surrogate prior with its own inductive bias. ASAP co-designs agent and system for wall-clock-centered hyperparameter optimization. **[HPO, agent, ML systems]**

### BrainAgent: LLM-Driven Multi-Agent Framework for Brain Signal Understanding

## 💡 Why It Matters

-   Both OpenAI and Anthropic releases are now subject to government vetting — a structural shift in AI governance. The era of unrestricted frontier model access is ending; the new paradigm is state-mediated trust.

-   ArXiv this week is heavy on agent safety: execution-time alignment kernels, decoupled pen-testing evaluation, and decentralized trust protocols. The field is moving from "can agents do X?" to "can we control agents doing X?" — a maturity signal.
