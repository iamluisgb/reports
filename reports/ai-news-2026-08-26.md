## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### Don't Overthink, Don't Underthink: Toward Adaptive Reasoning in Agentic AI

Argues that agentic systems need reasoning budgets that adapt to evolving task demands rather than fixed tokens or up-front difficulty estimates, and traces over- and under-reasoning failure modes on MATH-500 and GAIA. **[agents] [inference] [eval]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26442v1)

### Defending Wearable VLMs Against Private Attribute Inference

TGAP learns a residual transformation of visual tokens before they leave the trusted device boundary, cutting private-attribute accuracy from 56.7% to 7.4% on a 3,221-record privacy-utility benchmark while holding relaxed utility at 74.4%. **[privacy] [VLM] [inference]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28691v1)

### FaithSieve: Fine-Grained Evaluation of Math Proofs with Faithful Formal Evidence

Decomposes natural-language proofs into local reasoning units and gates Lean evidence on semantic alignment, reaching 81.4% exact first-error accuracy on a 350-problem Olympiad set versus 72.3% for direct judging. **[reasoning] [eval] [formal-methods]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26310v1)

### VBVR-Pro: A Scalable and Verifiable Suite for Native Visual Reasoning

A 300-task procedurally generated testbed with rule-grounded reward scorers; across 30+ generators, video holds up best when tasks require persistent spatiotemporal state tracking. **[visual-reasoning] [RL] [benchmarks]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26105v1)

### LowRankArena: A Standardized Evaluation Platform for SVD-Based LLM Compression

Unifies compression budgets, comparison regimes and inference measurement for low-rank LLM compression; an aligned audit of five SVD methods finds leaders shift by backbone and that multiple-choice accuracy can hide large perplexity degradation. **[compression] [eval] [inference]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26389v1)

## Why It Matters

-   Two flash-tier Chinese model releases — GLM-5.3-Flash and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next — landed the same day, and a third (Z.ai's Ox Alpha) got a weights-release confirmation. The competitive ground has moved from frontier capability claims to the small, cheap tier that most production traffic actually runs on.

-   Mechanical Turk's shutdown removes one of the oldest sources of on-demand human labeling, just as the paper slate here pushes toward verifiable, rule-grounded evaluation and reward signals — the direction is away from cheap crowd labels and toward automatically checkable tasks.
