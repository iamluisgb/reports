## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### Solvita: Enhancing LLMs for Competitive Programming via Agentic Evolution

LLMs struggle with hard competitive programming. Solvita uses agentic evolution to iteratively improve LLM performance through multi-agent self-play and verification, bridging the reliability gap of stateless frameworks. **[agents, LLM]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15301)

### Verifiable Agentic Infrastructure: Proof-Derived Authorization for Sovereign AI Systems

Autonomous AI agents can generate syntactically valid but semantically unsafe actions. Introduces DTF (Distributed Trust Framework) with Justification Proofs and Evidence Chains to shift authorization from standing identity to proof-derived authority. **[agents, security]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15228)

### NIMO Controller: Self-driving laboratory orchestrator based on MCP

Self-driving laboratories accelerate scientific discovery but are technically demanding. NIMO uses Model Context Protocol to make SDL orchestration accessible through a framework that reduces development complexity. **[agents, Model]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15227)

### Context Pruning for Coding Agents via Multi-Rubric Latent Reasoning

LLM coding agents waste most of their token budget on irrelevant code. Multi-rubric latent reasoning prunes context more effectively than single-objective sequence labelers, preserving task-relevant information. **[agents, LLM]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15315)

### STAR: Stage-attributed Triage and Repair for RCA Agents in Microservices

LLM-based root cause analysis agents for microservice AIOps are fragile — errors cascade through evidence collection, hypothesis, and diagnosis. STAR introduces stage-attributed triage to isolate and repair failures at each RCA stage. **[agents, SRE]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15581)

### SaaS-Bench: Can Computer-Use Agents Leverage Real-World SaaS?

Benchmark for computer-use agents on real-world SaaS tasks. Tests whether agents can actually leverage professional workflows in live SaaS environments, not just synthetic tasks. **[agents, Benchmark]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15777)

### Formal Methods Meet LLMs: Auditing, Monitoring, and Intervention for AI Compliance

Examines AI governance through formal methods — monitoring and auditing AI products throughout the development lifecycle, from pre-deployment testing to post-deployment auditing. **[security, LLM]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15445)

### Quantization Undoes Alignment: Bias Emergence in Compressed LLMs

LLMs are compressed via post-training quantization for cloud/edge deployment, but quantization can undo alignment — bias emerges in compressed models. Critical finding for any deployment of quantized LLMs. **[LLM, Benchmark]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15217)

### Fair outputs, Biased Internals: Latent Bias in LLMs

Instruction-tuned LLMs exhibit behavioral fairness in outputs while retaining biased associations internally. Shows suppressed representations can still affect model outputs under certain conditions. **[LLM, security]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15215)

### Zero-Shot Goal Recognition with LLMs

LLMs reach near-parity with classical planners on planning domains through world-knowledge exploitation rather than genuine symbolic reasoning. Goal recognition as an abdication test. **[LLM]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15333)

### Belief Engine: Configurable and Inspectable Stance Dynamics in Multi-Agent LLM Deliberation

LLM-based agents are increasingly used to simulate deliberative interactions such as negotiation, conflict resolution, and multi-turn opinion exchange. Yet generated transcripts often do not reveal why an agent's stance changes. **[agents, LLM]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15343)

### Imperfect World Models are Exploitable

Proposes a novel definition of model exploitation in reinforcement learning. Informally, a world model is exploitable if it implies that one policy should be strictly preferred over another while the environment's true transition model implies the opposite. **[agents]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15960)

### Deterministic Event-Graph Substrates as World Models for Counterfactual Reasoning

Studies event-graph substrates: a class of world models that represent agent state as an append-only log of typed RDF triples and answer counterfactual queries by forking the log under a structured intervention vocabulary. **[agents, inference]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15967)

### ShopGym: Integrated Framework for Realistic Simulation and Scalable Benchmarking of E-Commerce Web Agents

Developing and evaluating e-commerce web agents requires environments that preserve meaningful task structure while enabling controllable, reproducible, and scalable scientific comparison. **[agents, Benchmark]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16116)
