## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### SWIM: Student Writing Simulation via Proficiency-Conditioned Generation

Frames student writing simulation as proficiency-conditioned essay generation: SFT and RL with a proficiency-alignment reward beat prompting, but authentic low-proficiency writing remains hard to reproduce. **[LLM, generation, eval]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03215v1)

### MemoryLACE: Memory Lifecycle-Aware Consolidation and Evidence Retrieval

A lightweight long-term agent memory that models merge, supersession and contradiction relations across atomic memories, cutting BEAM runtime by 66.6% versus a reflective-memory baseline. **[agents, memory, RAG]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03201v1)

### BASP: Communication-Efficient Batch-Aware Sequence Parallelism for LLM Training

Batch-aware sequence parallelism partitions GPUs into disjoint groups by micro-batch size, cutting all-to-all cost for 1.17–1.31× faster Llama and Qwen training with unchanged accuracy. **[training, parallelism, infra]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03151v1)

### Counterfactual Fairness Audits of Multi-Step Clinical LLM Agents Require a Measured Per-Action Instability Floor

Re-running identical clinical vignettes changed an agent's action in 8.7% of cells, so the authors argue fairness audits must report a per-action instability floor before any disparity claim. **[agents, eval, fairness]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03221v2)

### Evaluating Large Language Models for Forced Outage Risk Prediction

Zero-shot LLMs trail supervised classifiers on macro-F1 and precision for weather-related grid outages, but offer complementary reasoning and geographic scalability. **[LLM, forecasting, eval]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04272v1)
