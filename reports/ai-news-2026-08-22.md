## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### Lexical Perturbations Disrupt LLM Reasoning: An Empirical Study of Attention Diversion

Character-level noise fragments subword tokenization, and the fragments attract disproportionate attention mass in middle and final layers — damage that CoT prompting, spell-checking and self-repair fail to consistently undo. **[robustness, attention, eval]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22140v1)

### Decoupled Physical Modeling and Execution for Physics Reasoning

A two-stage post-training framework has small VLMs build an explicit model of the physical system before calculating, beating GRPO by ~3% on average across PhysReason, PhyX and SeePhys. **[reasoning, VLM, post-training]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22126v2)

### Development and Feasibility Evaluation of an Edge AI as Medical Device System for Breast Cancer Multidisciplinary Team Meetings

A fully on-device Whisper plus MedGemma-RAG pipeline on a single Jetson AGX Orin transcribes tumor-board meetings and grounds recommendations in NICE guidance, keeping patient audio and outputs inside institutional infrastructure. **[on-device, RAG, healthcare]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22108v1)

### Task-Driven 3D Printability Assistance via Geometry- and Knowledge-Grounded LLM Reasoning

Grounding LLM reasoning in geometry evidence and structured material knowledge predicts printability, material and process before fabrication, lifting Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite material-selection accuracy from 37.5% to 90.0%. **[grounding, LLM, manufacturing]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22128v1)

### More Accurate or More Efficient? Evaluating Locally Deployed Compact Open-Weight Language Models for Mathematical Reasoning

A controlled benchmark of Gemma3:4b, Phi3:3.8b and Qwen3:4b finds no accuracy winner — and shows Gemma3:4b returns roughly three times more correct answers per watt-hour than Qwen3:4b. **[local-LLM, benchmarking, efficiency]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22048v1)

## Why It Matters

-   Local model quality is as much about serving as about weights: the day's top AI thread and the compact-model paper both point to setup — quantization, tokenization, runtime — as the gap between benchmark numbers and lived experience.

-   Silent behavior changes erode trust: reports of Claude Code A/B testing effort levels and Meta's trial disclosures both show why users and regulators increasingly expect changes to be announced, not discovered.
