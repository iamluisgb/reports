### Mitigating Perceptual Judgment Bias in Multimodal LLM-as-a-Judge

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02578)

### AdaCodec: A Predictive Visual Code for Video MLLMs

Video is temporally redundant: adjacent frames usually share most objects, background, and layout. Yet existing video multimodal large language models usually encode each sampled frame independently. **[llm, code]**

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02569)

### ClinEnv: An Interactive Multi-Stage Long Horizon EHR Environment for Agents

Clinical practice is not the selection of an answer from enumerated options: a physician gathers heterogeneous information incrementally and commits to sequential, irreversible decisions under uncertainty. **[agent]**
