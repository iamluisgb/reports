## 💡 Why It Matters

-   The AI infrastructure arms race is accelerating: SoftBank ($85B), Anthropic ($965B valuation), and OpenRouter ($113M) are all placing massive bets on compute and inference routing. For SREs and engineers, this means more tooling, more complexity, and more demand for optimization.

-   The counter-narrative is gaining traction: domain expertise > model size, open-source agents > vendor lock-in, and AI cost scrutiny is peaking. The era of "just throw more GPUs at it" may be waning — efficiency and domain depth are the new moats.
