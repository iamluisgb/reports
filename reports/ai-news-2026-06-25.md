## Why It Matters

-   The Anthropic-Alibaba dispute is the first major IP case in the model era. How courts handle "capability extraction" will define the rules of engagement for model deployment globally — and could reshape how frontier labs license their models.

-   OpenAI's custom chip + Qualcomm's Modular acquisition + NVIDIA's 45°C cooling = the AI infrastructure stack is fragmenting vertically. Inference is moving from cloud-only to edge, custom silicon, and liquid-cooled hyperscale. The SRE who understands this full stack will be irreplaceable.

-   Agent safety papers are proliferating — the Unfireable Safety Kernel paper's core insight (controls inside the agent's address space are fundamentally subvertible) should be mandatory reading for anyone building agentic systems. Externalize your guardrails.
