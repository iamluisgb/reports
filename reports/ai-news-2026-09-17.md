## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### AutoTuneBench: Trustworthy Measurement for Agent Auto-Tuning of LLM Serving Engines

Agents that auto-tune kernels and serving engines run a propose-measure-keep loop, and the measurements inside it are routinely untrustworthy. From a four-day pilot corpus of 619 model calls, the authors catalogue four failure modes — strawman baselines that manufacture speedups, timings that don't transfer across machines, saturated tasks that nullify comparisons, infrastructure defects impersonating science. Their fix is protocol-as-code: a database-level validator rejects out-of-protocol results, anti-cheat checks sit outside the agent's modification surface, and paired-seed statistics anchor to a 5% cross-run coefficient of variation. **[agents, benchmarking, systems]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18123v1)

### The Other Half of the Memory Wall: Serving 35B MoEs from SSD with Trained Routing Prediction

MoE inference on consumer hardware is bounded by weight bytes — a 35B model is 19.5GB at 4-bit — and naive SSD offloading fails because layer N+1's experts must be chosen before layer N's output exists, so reads can't hide behind compute. Edge0 adds a prerouter that predicts the next layer's routing one token ahead and consumes the prediction as the routing itself, with an unmerged recovery LoRA paying back the int4 quality loss. On a single 24GB machine it serves a 35B MoE at 20 tok/s inside 3GiB of peak active memory. **[inference, moe, quantisation]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18063v1)

### Market Signal Injection: Adversarial Context Manipulation of LLM Pricing Agents

LLM pricing agents react to how market data is presented even when the numbers are unchanged. Market signal injection manipulates numerical formatting, competitor ordering or qualitative commentary — no explicit instructions — against nine open-weight models in simulated Bertrand duopolies and triopolies plus three proprietary models. Sentiment-based attacks produce the largest shifts, which propagate to other firms and move both profits and consumer surplus — and larger models are not consistently more robust. **[agents, security, economics]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18357v1)

### Recursive Reasoning or Statistical Extrapolation? In-Context Learning in Multi-Agent Interdependent Decision-Making

It is unclear whether LLM agents improving in context are refining their reasoning or merely extrapolating statistical patterns from interaction history. In public goods games requiring recursive belief reasoning, scored against a history-independent rational-expectations benchmark, disrupting the statistical structure of the feedback makes the benefit of longer context vanish — decision quality degrades to the no-context baseline. The collapse is sharply amplified by stronger strategic interdependence. **[agents, evaluation, in-context-learning]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18591v1)

### A Four-Stage Decomposition of Word-Problem Solving and Mechanistic Fragility in LLM Math Reasoning

Models ace grade-school word problems yet a single irrelevant clause can collapse accuracy. The authors decompose the internal computation into a four-stage pipeline — Schema Abstraction, Operation Planning, Operand Binding, Computation — each stage landing in an identifiable band of layers. Distractor-induced failure localises to Operation Planning alone, implemented by attention heads whose causal role is validated bidirectionally. **[interpretability, reasoning, robustness]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17804v1)

## Why It Matters

-   Half of today's news is claims, and the papers amount to a kit for auditing them. AutoTuneBench's four failure modes — strawman baselines, timings that don't transfer, saturated tasks — read as a checklist to run against QoRL's 81%-faster-than-Postgres result, which ships a headline number without a frozen protocol or an external validator. HarnessTax isolating harness from weights and Xiaomi streaming RL curves live are the same instinct applied elsewhere — publish the measurement apparatus, not just the measurement. The results worth building on are the ones that survive re-measurement.

-   Three of today's papers converge on an uncomfortable point — the competence we benchmark is extrapolation over surface statistics, and surface statistics are precisely what adversaries and odd inputs perturb. MSI moves pricing agents with formatting and sentiment while the numbers stay fixed, and scale confers no immunity; the ICL result collapses to baseline once the history's statistical structure is disrupted; one distractor clause breaks word problems through a single stage of attention heads. Against that substrate, Cloudflare ships an audit-agent skill and OpenAI a misalignment framework — actuation is racing ahead of reliability. Treat agent autonomy as proportional to the quality of the checks around it, not the parameter count.

-   The common fix across the day is architectural trust — protocols frozen as code with validators outside the agent's reach, per AutoTuneBench, and verified restore paths per the AWS admission and the backups piece. The data at the struck facilities wasn't lost to bad luck — it was lost to replication that was never tested against recovery. Nvidia pulling Rust into CUDA and GLM building its own serving stack are the same reflex one layer down: when you own the substrate, you can actually make promises about it. The consolidation to watch is not of models but of measurement and serving infrastructure.
