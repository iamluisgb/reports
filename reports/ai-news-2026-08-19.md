## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### HYDRA: A Heterogeneous Chiplet DSE Framework for Serving Dynamic Hybrid LLM Workloads

Jointly explores chiplet composition, placement, bandwidth, batching and scheduling for hybrid Transformer-Mamba serving, reporting 1.55x throughput and 43.7% lower time-to-first-token versus state-of-the-art baselines. **[inference, hardware, serving]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19395v1)

### SPADE: Self-Play in Adaptive Synthetic Executable Environments

A self-play RL framework where one LLM writes its own Gym-style training environments as executable code while a reasoning agent learns in them; at 30B scale it adds +5.3 across eight held-out benchmarks and +13.9 on ACEBench-Agent. **[agents, RL, self-play]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19197v3)

### Beyond the Transcript: Detecting Covert Co ordination in Latent Multi-Agent Communication

Introduces a monitor for agents that coordinate through hidden latent states instead of public text, reaching 0.993 AUROC on controlled auctions and cutting collusive low-bid behavior by 47.3 points via counterfactual steering. **[multi-agent, safety, monitoring]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19161v1)

### Accelerated Genetic Programming Hyper-Heuristics for Simulation-Based Scheduling via Agentic AI

Guided agentic refactoring with Claude on real HPC scheduling simulations cut runtime from 1,298s to under 200s with unchanged outputs, saving four million core-hours (NZ$320,000) annually. **[agents, code, HPC]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19487v1)

### Are LLMs becoming similarly creative? Evidence from three years of models

Analyzing three years of releases on open-ended prompts and the Alternate Uses Task, outputs grow significantly less diverse over time, suggesting models are converging in creative substance. **[eval, creativity, benchmark]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19437v1)

## Why It Matters

-   OpenRouter folding into Stripe is consolidation at the middle of the LLM stack: model routing stops being an independent business and becomes a feature of the payments layer. Watch who absorbs the next API-economy intermediary.

-   Homework scores that rise while exam scores fall, and creative outputs that converge across models, are the same failure mode: what is easy to measure keeps improving while what is hard to measure quietly degrades. Evaluation design is becoming the bottleneck.
