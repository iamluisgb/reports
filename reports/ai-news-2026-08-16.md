## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### SEER: Long-Context Reasoning via Selective Visual-Text Compression

Renders text into images to cut token counts, but scans visually and retrieves raw text only where precision demands it — on LongBench it beats the Glyph-9B visual baseline by 2.33 points while keeping prompt savings. **[long-context, multimodal, compression]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15962v1)

### Ask to Be Sure: Informative Interactions for Confident Multi-Turn LLM Recommendation

Rewards each clarifying question by how much it reduces the entropy of the model’s recommendations, fine-tuning a conversational recommender to gather better preferences — gains on INSPIRED and ReDial without ground-truth labels. **[recommendation, conversational, RL]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15949v2)

### Beyond Visual CoT: Internalized Visual Thinking for Proactive Video Reasoning

Trains a video model to predict latent representations of future frames during post-training, then answer directly at inference — matching explicit visual chain-of-thought accuracy with over 5x lower end-to-end latency. **[multimodal, video, inference]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15869v1)

### LLMs Get Smarter from Targeted Synthetic Multilingual Data

HOTFIXR probes a student model’s per-language weaknesses and generates targeted multilingual synthetic data, adding 6.2% on in-distribution tasks while reducing forgetting on unseen tasks and languages by 3.7% and 7.1%. **[multilingual, synthetic-data, training]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15964v1)

### RLCascadeRouter: Quality-Estimator-Free Cascade Routing via Reinforcement Learning

Formulates LLM cascade routing as a Markov decision process that reconsiders stopping or escalating after each response, optimizing the cost-quality trade-off directly — better trade-offs across ten LLMRouterBench benchmarks and no retraining to admit unseen models. **[inference, routing, RL]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15817v1)

## Why It Matters

-   The watermark fight and “kidney disappointment” are the same story from two angles: generated text is now being policed — by vendors embedding marks and by filters catching laundered wording — and the line between AI assistance and silent adulteration is being settled in public, in real time.

-   Stripe reportedly paying $7B+ for OpenRouter signals that model gateways — the plumbing that routes traffic and billing between providers — have graduated from dev tooling to payments-scale infrastructure.
