## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### SCENARIODIFF: Scenario-level Guidance for Multimodal Time Series Forecasting

Hierarchical agents turn noisy documents into historical evidence, scenario descriptions and sparse anchor points that condition a multimodal diffusion transformer for event-driven forecasting. **[forecasting, multimodal, diffusion]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17164v1)

### Towards Safer RAG: Only Agents Capable of System 2 Thinking may Access Untrusted Documents

Finds that reasoning-capable models are substantially more robust to knowledge-poisoning in RAG, supporting a relaxed Cordon Principle where only System 2 agents read untrusted documents. **[RAG, security, reasoning]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17153v1)

### Looped Language Models Improve Compositional Tool Calling

Recurrent-depth models beat matched non-looped baselines on multi-step tool use across API-Bank, BFCL and NESTful, with adaptive inference giving the best compute-performance trade-off. **[agents, tool-use, inference]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18171v1)

### Q-Learning With World Models

QWM runs test-time search over imagined trajectories on top of standard Q-learning while training only on real transitions, avoiding compounding model bias and beating prior state of the art on Robomimic and LIBERO. **[RL, world-models, robotics]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17163v1)

### Uncertainty-Aware Decision Making in Multimodal Large Language Models

A decision-centered survey of MLLM uncertainty: token-level and semantic signals, calibration, and actions like abstention, clarification, retrieval and escalation under weak multimodal evidence. **[survey, calibration, multimodal]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17084v1)
