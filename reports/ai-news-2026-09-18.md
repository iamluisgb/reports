## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### How Do Agent Harnesses Create Value? Planning Information and Release Control in Stateful LLM Agents

Which harness component earns its keep — planning guidance, execution scaffolding, or completion checks — is usually asserted, never priced. Across 265 matched cells on Retail and Airline tasks, real task plans lift oracle-verified success by 7.17 percentage points over word-matched sham text (90% CI 1.15–13.36), with gains concentrated on harder tasks. A read-only terminal verifier rejects 61% of oracle-invalid episodes while withholding 17% of correct ones at under a cent each — and which lever dominates depends on the loss assigned to erroneous acceptance, with planning winning at low liability. **agent-harnesses, evaluation, verification**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20474v1)

### SoL-Pi: Recursively Scaling Auto-Research Loops for Efficient Agent Harness

When coding agents run unattended around the clock, token efficiency of the harness — not the model call — becomes the bottleneck for recursive self-improvement. Scaling auto-research loops across increasingly diverse environments lets rollout performance select harness mechanisms, and four survive: action execution, context compaction, observation handling, and delegated reading. On 51-task EdgeBench the resulting SoL-Pi matches Pi across GPT-5.6 Sol and Opus 5 while cutting recorded token use, and the improvements transfer beyond the environments that produced them. **agents, recursive-self-improvement, token-efficiency**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20519v1)

### Compositional Reasoning in Language Models under Reinforcement Learning Post-Training

RL post-training is assumed to teach models to recombine learned skills on novel problems — the point of compositional reasoning when training data is finite. Formalising composition as dependency graphs over data-structure tasks with deterministic rewards, the authors find a consistent decomposed-to-composed asymmetry: training on decomposed skills does not reliably transfer to composed tasks, while composed-task training transfers readily back down. If your eval suite tests skills in isolation, RL gains may systematically overstate what the model can actually assemble. **reinforcement-learning, compositional-reasoning, generalisation**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19465v1)

### LearnActCoder: Role-Aware Error Memory for Adaptive Clinical Coding Agents

Clinical coding agents repeat the same failure modes — unsupported codes, missed conditions, specificity errors — and fine-tuning is often off the table in regulated settings. Learn-Then-Act converts a small labelled error batch into a structured Mistake Knowledge Database, routing false-negative lessons to a recall-oriented Coder and false-positive lessons to a precision-oriented Judge. On 150 matched MIMIC-III notes the structured memory lifts CPT F1 by 5.9 percentage points — raw-example and reflection-style memories sit near the no-memory baseline, so structure rather than volume is what makes error memory pay. **agents, error-memory, clinical-coding**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19721v1)

### MTVA-Bench: Evaluating the Language Model Inside Cascaded Voice Agents

Most deployed voice agents are cascades — ASR transcribes, an LM decides and calls tools, TTS speaks — yet the LM making every decision is measured either end-to-end, where recognition errors pollute the score, or on generic LLM benchmarks that miss what makes phone calls hard. MTVA-Bench tests the LM under in-cascade conditions: noisy transcripts, a caller split across multiple messages, and replies that must honour a specified language and script. It isolates decision quality from transcription quality — the number that actually moves when you swap the middle model. **voice-agents, evaluation, benchmarking**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20152v1)

## Why It Matters

-   The harness has become the load-bearing component, and this batch prices it. Real task plans beat word-matched sham text by 7.17 points on oracle-verified success, and a sub-cent terminal verifier rejects 61% of invalid episodes while blocking 17% of good ones. Bend's 490-point traction for proof-guarded execution and OpenSpec's climb are the same wager in production tooling — engineer the accept/reject boundary, not the prompt.

-   Post-training has a composition problem that isolated evals hide — decomposed-skill training fails to transfer upward, so composed behaviour has to come from the harness or from training on composed tasks directly. SoL-Pi treats that assembly as the optimisation target, evolving harness mechanisms until they transfer beyond their development environments. LearnActCoder supplies the storage layer — structured mistake databases gain 5.9 CPT F1 points while reflection-style memory sits at baseline, because unstructured memory decays into noise and structured lessons compound.

-   The remaining headlines price the commoditisation running underneath. Bonsai 2's near-lossless 9× compression and a Qwen 3.8 27B at 13.1 GB VRAM push inference toward commodity hardware, while Astra for Law's 593 comments and a Microsoft executive calling scraping 'the largest theft of labor in human history' locate the durable value in verticals and in the labour that produced the weights. The OpenAI breach — a heap overflow chained through an SSO misconfiguration into internal repos — completes the picture: the harness that creates value is also the attack surface.
