## Why It Matters

-   The July 2026 model wave (Sonnet 5, GPT-5.6, Grok 4.5, DeepSeek V4) confirms frontier competition is accelerating, not consolidating. The improvement margin remains wide and real differentiation plays out in post-training, not base architecture.

-   Agent swarms and the economics of small models running in parallel (Cursor, the PlanFlip attack vector) indicate production architecture is migrating from single-frontier to multi-agent orchestration — exactly where Quiron operates.

-   The $1.65T hidden debt and SB 315 regulation point to an industry of massive investment with systemic risk — but also regulatory maturation. The cost of capital for AI is rising.
