## Papers — ArXiv CS.AI

### Generating Biomedical Fact-Checking Reports with RL-Enhanced Agentic Search

BioCheck Agent writes structured biomedical fact-checking reports instead of bare labels, searching PubMed with Boolean operators; EG-GRPO reinforcement learning lifts label accuracy on SciFact by 9.95% over Qwen3.5-4B and cuts evidence hallucination by 19.63%. **[agents, RAG, eval]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23811v1)

### MARS: Multi-Specialist LLM Relay System for Competitive Programming

A prompt-only relay of topic specialists (DP, graphs, strings, geometry) grounded by retrieval over an algorithm-theory corpus; on CodeContests with Gemma 4 it reaches 0.624 pass rate, +14.4 points over direct prompting at lower wall-clock cost. **[agents, code-gen, RAG]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23918v1)

### Retrieval-augmented generation vs. deterministic tax computation in multi-agent financial advisory

A 2x2 factorial experiment finds enabling the tax optimization engine significantly reduced savings, while the RAG effect was not significant — the model's internalized finance knowledge performed competently without explicit tooling. **[RAG, agents, eval]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23908v1)

### Beyond the Mandate: A Systematic Security Analysis of the Agent Payments Protocol (AP2)

Threat modeling of AP2 v0.2 over five deployment architectures yields 48 threats in five attack families, eight rated High; valid mandate signatures alone do not guarantee the transaction matches user intent when pre-authorization context is manipulated. **[agents, security, protocol]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23858v1)

### PuzzleKV: Page-Wise Low-Rank Decomposition for KV Cache Compression

Training- and calibration-free page-wise low-rank decomposition keeps more than 96% of full-KV performance at ~60% of the original cache storage, and 93% with quantization at 18.7%. **[inference, efficiency, kv-cache]**

Sources: [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23843v1)
