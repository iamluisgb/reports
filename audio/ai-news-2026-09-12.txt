[HOST_B] Ecdysis attacks the same territory from the plumbing side. Instead of evolving agent harnesses by brute-force search, it trains them: it aggregates failures across task instances to separate model-specific misses from systematic harness defects, and then repairs only the harness. Up to one point eight four times faster harness training, and reasoning accuracy up eighteen point five six percent over prior harness-evolution methods. My reading: a lot of what we call agent failure was never the model — it was the scaffolding, and this paper says repair the scaffolding and leave the model alone.
